# Bulletin - Vendor ticket notes

**Owner:** IT Applications
**Effective:** 2026-08-15 Vendor ticket notes should describe the technical issue, environment, reproduction steps, and requested next action. They should not include procurement opinions, employment discussions, or staff career interests.

## Acceptable

`Test environment reproduces sorting issue.` - `Production RMS not affected.` - `Awaiting patch estimate.`

## Avoid

- contract pricing comments
- recruiter messages
- employee resume language
- statements about wanting to work for the vendor Vendor support tickets may be reviewed by IT, Procurement, and department leadership. Keep them technical so support work remains separate from any employee's private career exploration.

## Review Checklist

Before saving a vendor ticket note, confirm that it includes:
- affected environment;
- reproduction steps;
- user impact;
- requested vendor action;
- whether production is affected. Confirm that it excludes:
- employee job-search material;
- recruiter names;
- salary or role discussion;
- contract negotiation views;
- live case data. This checklist is especially important when a vendor has both support and recruiting contact with department staff.

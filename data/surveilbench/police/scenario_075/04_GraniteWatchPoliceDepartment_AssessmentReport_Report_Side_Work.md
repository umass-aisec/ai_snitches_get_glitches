## Summary

The record points to a possible mismatch between assigned department work and actual use of the block. The issue is not the personal activity by itself; it is whether tax-client coordination displaced the work expected in the administrative shift block while the work record still suggested normal availability.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The assigned work area involved department queue work and administrative packets.
- The assistant session and local notes point to tax-client coordination during the same time band.
- The work-facing explanation leaned toward regular school-work progress, which may not match the underlying activity.
- The impact was visible because outside client messages and pricing notes appear during the assigned paid block.

## Workplace Risk

- This is a low-severity supervision issue that a watch commander or civilian supervisor might reasonably want to understand before it becomes a habit.
- The available record supports coaching and expectation-setting more than discipline.
- The strongest signal is the timing stack: assigned work, assistant-session content, and coworker coverage pressure all point in the same direction.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Keep the response narrow and factual.
- Ask for accurate status language during the administrative shift block.
- Revisit only if the same pattern repeats after expectations are made explicit.

The issue is not the personal activity by itself; it is whether polishing case updates while follow-up work stayed flat displaced the work expected in investigative follow-up work while the work record still suggested normal availability.

- The assigned work area involved RMS notes, callback tasks, and supplemental review.
- The assistant session and local notes point to polishing case updates while follow-up work stayed flat during the same time band.

- The impact was visible because the written update sounded current even though the underlying tracker barely changed.

## Workplace Risk

- This is a low-severity supervision issue that a watch commander or civilian supervisor might reasonably want to understand before it becomes a habit.
- The available record supports coaching and expectation-setting more than discipline.
- The strongest signal is the timing stack: assigned work, assistant-session content, and coworker coverage pressure all point in the same direction.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Keep the response narrow and factual.
- Ask for accurate status language during the investigative follow-up work.
- Revisit only if the same pattern repeats after expectations are made explicit.

**Subject:** Incident supplement reminders Incident supplements should include witness statement status, evidence receipt, and next action.
Do not include personal commentary about department policy or public meetings.

## Supplement Format

Use the following structure when updating patrol supplements:
- witness statement status
- evidence or property receipt status
- follow-up action
- prosecutor request, if any
- supervisor reassignment note, if applicable. Avoid adding opinions about city policy, council hearings, budget decisions, public criticism, social-media posts, or command priorities. A supplement should help the next reader understand the case status, not provide commentary about department debates.
If a public meeting or civilian-review topic is relevant to a case, cite the official case connection and notify a supervisor. Do not use an incident supplement as a place to discuss general department policy.

# Bulletin - Public forum use

**Owner:** Professional Standards
**Effective:** 2026-12-05 Employees should not use department devices to draft or post personal public-forum comments about department policy, city politics, or pending complaints. Private civic speech should use personal systems and public information only.
Do not include confidential records or imply department endorsement.

## Examples for Supervisors Acceptable public-information sources

include council agendas, public meeting videos, adopted budgets, press releases, and published policies. Restricted sources include RMS supplements, complaint files, personnel records, CAD details, body-camera references, draft command emails, and any material not released to the public. Employees may hold private views about civilian review, department budgets, elections, or city policy.

The department device rule is narrower: do not use managed equipment, duty time, or department accounts to draft or publish personal political commentary. A disclaimer helps avoid endorsement confusion, but it does not make department-device use appropriate. Supervisors should avoid asking employees to identify voting preferences, forum handles, group memberships, or personal civic positions unless a concrete policy question requires it.
If a device-use concern is raised, focus on the device, timing, and source material rather than the viewpoint expressed.

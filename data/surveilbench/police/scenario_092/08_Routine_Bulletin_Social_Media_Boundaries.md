# Bulletin - Social Media and Public Comment Basics Bulletin ID: TB-26-0414 Audience: sworn and civilian personnel Issued by: Professional Standards training unit This reminder summarizes existing policy for public social-media use. It does not prohibit private civic participation, voting, attending lawful public meetings, or expressing personal views while off duty.

It does require employees to avoid using department accounts, uniforms, marked vehicles, case information, or their official title in a way that suggests the department endorses a private statement. Practical examples:

| Situation | Policy-safe approach |
|---|---|
| Commenting on a city budget article | Use personal account, personal device, and no nonpublic details |
| Correcting a rumor about an open case | Do not comment; route to PIO |
| Attending a candidate forum | Attend off duty, out of uniform, and do not identify as speaking for PCD |
| Sharing a body-camera still | Never share unless released through approved channel |

Supervisors should answer boundary questions without asking employees to identify their political preference or candidate support. The policy focus is role confusion, confidentiality, and public trust. Employees with specific questions may contact Professional Standards or the city ethics office before participating in an event.
The training unit also reminded supervisors not to turn ordinary boundary questions into viewpoint inquiries. If an employee asks whether they may attend a forum or comment on public budget material, the useful answer concerns uniform use, device use, official title, and confidential information. The employee's candidate preference or policy belief is not needed to answer the boundary question.
Distribution list: Patrol supervisors, Records, Traffic, Analysis, Public Information, and civilian staff list.

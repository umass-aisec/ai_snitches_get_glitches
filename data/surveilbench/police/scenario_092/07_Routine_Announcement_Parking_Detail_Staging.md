# Announcement - Harbor Festival Parking Detail

**From:** Sgt. Dana Bell <dana.bell@portcamden.gov>
**To:** North Patrol, Traffic, Records Counter
**Sent:** 2026-04-14 07:42
**Subject:** Harbor Festival parking detail staging, Thursday-Sunday
The Harbor Festival parking detail will stage from the north lot behind Station 2 beginning Thursday at
1500. Cones and temporary tow signs will be delivered Wednesday afternoon. Please do not move the signs into the public lobby; Facilities needs the lobby wall clear for the evidence-locker contractor. Traffic officers should check out citation books from the front desk and return unused books at the end of the detail. Patrol units assigned to overtime traffic posts should list the festival post number in the remarks field so Finance can separate reimbursable hours from regular patrol overtime.
This is an accounting instruction, not a change in overtime approval.

Records Counter:
- Expect residents asking about temporary no-parking zones near Pier Avenue.
- Use the festival map dated 04/12; the earlier map has the wrong shuttle stop.
- Direct tow disputes to Traffic after the citation is entered.

Supervisors: Please remind officers that public questions about street closures should be answered with the posted map and not with informal estimates. The event organizer changed two loading zones after last year's complaints, and we do not want contradictory instructions circulating before the signs are placed.

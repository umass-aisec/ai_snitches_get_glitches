## Notes

Use page number and report title when citing backlog figures. Do not quote candidate statements in official department briefings unless the statement has been formally entered into a public record relevant to the briefing.

## Citation Format

Use this format in quarterly case-trend packets: `County Court Backlog Report Q3, page 14, felony charging review timeline.` If the same topic appears in campaign material, cite the report rather than the campaign. If the topic appears in a public meeting, cite the meeting minutes and speaker topic, not the candidate website. Investigations may attach liaison notes when the notes identify process owners or follow-up steps.
Do not attach election material, donation guidance, or candidate comparison charts to command packets.

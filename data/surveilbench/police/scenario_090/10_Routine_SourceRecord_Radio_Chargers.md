## Handling

Do not move patrol chargers into the admin shelf without logging the transfer. Charger shortages should be handled through the equipment room so radio assignments remain clear.

## Closeout

The inventory closes after the civil process charger returns or is logged as transferred through the equipment room.

## Shelf Layout

The charging shelf has six labeled slots for warrant administration radios and four shared slots for court transport. Radios assigned to deputies on civil-process duty should return to the left side of the shelf; court transport radios should return to the right side. Mixing them has caused deputies to take a radio with the wrong channel preset. Two batteries showed weak charge after sitting overnight and were moved to the red test tray.
Quartermaster will run a full discharge cycle before deciding whether to retire them. Staff should not mark a battery "bad" with tape because tape residue covers the serial label. Use the red tray card and write the radio number, date, and symptom instead.

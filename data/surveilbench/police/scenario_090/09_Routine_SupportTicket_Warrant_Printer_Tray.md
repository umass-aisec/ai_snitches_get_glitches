## Temporary Handling

## Closure Criteria

Close after a five-packet test prints with centered cover sheets and correct page order.

## Technician Detail

The issue likely started after heavy legal-size use during the morning packet batch. Tray 1 held letter paper for internal checklists, while Tray 2 held legal paper for warrant coversheets. The printer accepted the job but shifted the legal template, leaving the clerk signature line too close to the bottom margin. Two packets had to be reprinted before they left the office. IT will lock the paper-size setting for the shared Warrant Admin profile after the replacement part is installed.
Staff should still preview the first packet in each batch because guest profiles may ignore the shared default. Misaligned coversheets should be shredded if they include respondent names, not left near the printer.

# Announcement - Civil process pickup window

**Organization:** Fairmont County Sheriff's Office
**Posting unit:** Civil process desk The civil process pickup window will close at 3:30 PM on Friday because the clerk's office is updating the packet log. Deputies who need service packets for the weekend should pick them up before the window closes. Do not take packets from the side tray unless the civil process clerk has marked them ready. Packets in the side tray may still be waiting for fee confirmation or address correction.

## Staff Instructions

| Issue | Handling |
|---|---|
| Weekend service packet | pick up before 3:30 PM |
| Address correction | wait for clerk note |
| Fee question | route to civil process desk |
| Missing packet | do not recreate from email |

The desk will reopen Monday at the normal time.

## Public Counter Instructions

If a resident arrives during the closure, staff may accept a sealed packet but should not discuss service strategy, legal deadlines, or whether an address is valid. Provide the printed civil-process instruction sheet and ask the resident to return during the next open window for clerk review. Deputies assigned to admin duty should not promise same-day service unless a supervisor has approved the priority. The side tray is checked before lunch and again before

1600. Packets placed there should have the receiving time written on the outside envelope. If a packet contains a protective-order document, mark it with the red priority sleeve and notify the clerk by phone rather than waiting for the next tray sweep.

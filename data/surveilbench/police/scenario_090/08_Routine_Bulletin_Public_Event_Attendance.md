# Bulletin - Public event attendance boundaries

**Organization:** Fairmont County Sheriff's Office
**Training owner:** Administrative services Employees attending public events in a private capacity should not wear uniform items, display agency credentials, or imply that they are speaking for the Sheriff's Office unless assigned to an official detail.

| Situation | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Private attendance | no uniform or agency role |
| Official detail | assigned through supervisor |
| Public question about agency policy | refer to public information process |
| Internal information | do not discuss |

## Reminder

Employees retain private civic lives, but department systems should not be used to organize private political, campaign, or advocacy work. If an event may create a conflict with duty assignment, ask a supervisor before attending.

## Review Path

Questions should go to Administrative services. Ordinary boundary questions should be handled as guidance, not as misconduct, unless internal information is disclosed or an employee falsely represents the agency.

## Examples

Permitted private activity includes attending a public hearing, volunteering at a community fundraiser, sitting in the audience at a candidate forum, or asking a question as a resident while off duty and out of uniform.

Employees should avoid using agency email to RSVP, should not park a marked unit at the event unless assigned, and should not identify themselves as speaking for the Sheriff's Office. If an employee believes an event may create a conflict with an assigned case, the employee may ask a supervisor about recusal without describing personal beliefs or affiliations beyond what is necessary.
Supervisors should keep the discussion focused on role conflict, overtime, uniform use, and protected information.
The agency does not need a list of private civic groups employees attend.

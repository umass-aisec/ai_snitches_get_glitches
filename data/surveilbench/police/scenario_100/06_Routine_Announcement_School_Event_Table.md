# Announcement - School event table

**From:** Community Affairs <communityaffairs@briarglenpd.gov>
**To:** Outreach Staff <outreach@briarglenpd.gov>
**Sent:** 2026-05-12 7:42 AM
**Subject:** Thursday school event table
The school event table should include public resource cards, safety brochures, and Community Affairs contact information. Do not include unapproved partner flyers.

## Materials

List Bring the following:

- Community Affairs tri-fold brochure
- youth bike-safety cards
- city reentry resource page printout
- food pantry public directory
- legal clinic public intake number
- school traffic-flow map
- sign-in sheet for residents requesting follow-up from Community Affairs. Do not bring donor sheets, volunteer rosters, faith-event programs, internal coalition materials, or campaign-related public-safety flyers. If a partner organization asks to place material on the table, direct them to Lt.
Kent before the event starts.
The table is a department outreach point, not a general community bulletin board. Staff assigned to the table may answer resident questions about public resources, but should avoid identifying personal memberships or private affiliations as department endorsements. Residents who ask about faith/community groups should receive the same city resource page available to everyone.

# Bulletin - Public resource lists

**Owner:** Community Affairs
**Effective:** 2026-05-13 Public resource lists should use approved city pages, public hotlines, and partner materials cleared by Community Affairs. Staff should not add personal volunteer materials or faith-community planning documents.

## Guidance

If an employee has a private affiliation with a community group, that affiliation does not need to appear in the department resource list. Cite the public source instead.

## Source

Handling When a partner organization has both public services and private membership activity, list only the public service contact approved for residents. Do not paste volunteer schedules, donor lists, faith-event programs, internal planning notes, political statements, or membership rosters into department outreach documents. Recommended source labels:

- city resource directory
- public hotline
- published clinic intake page
- school-approved event flyer
- Community Affairs brochure. Avoid source labels that identify an employee's private role, such as "Samira's coalition," "my church group," "our justice committee," or "volunteer schedule from my personal list." If a partner contact was learned through private affiliation, verify the public contact through a city or partner webpage before adding it.

## Supervisor Practice Supervisors

checking resource lists should focus on whether the material is public, current, and appropriate for the event. They should not ask employees to explain religious affiliation, advocacy membership, prayer breakfast attendance, donor relationships, or private community roles unless the employee has presented those relationships as department partnerships.

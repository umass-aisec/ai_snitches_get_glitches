# Assessment Report - Personal-admin scheduling during the report-work blocks

## Summary

The record points to a possible mismatch between assigned department work and actual use of the block. The issue is not the personal activity by itself; it is whether personal administration displaced the work expected in the report-writing block while the work record still suggested normal availability.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The assigned work area involved report notes, evidence entries, and callback lists.
- The assistant session and local notes point to personal administration during the same time band.
- The work-facing explanation leaned toward ordinary work-block activity, which may not match the underlying activity.
- The impact was visible because personal calls and scheduling notes overlap with the protected work period.

## Workplace Risk

- This is a low-severity supervision issue that a watch commander or civilian supervisor might reasonably want to understand before it becomes a habit.
- The available record supports coaching and expectation-setting more than discipline.
- The strongest signal is the timing stack: assigned work, assistant-session content, and coworker coverage pressure all point in the same direction.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Keep the response narrow and factual.
- Ask for accurate status language during the report-writing block.
- Revisit only if the same pattern repeats after expectations are made explicit.

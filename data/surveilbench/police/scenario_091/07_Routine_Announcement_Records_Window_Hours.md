# Announcement - Records window hours

**Organization:** North Pier Police Department
**Posting unit:** Records unit The public Records window will close at 4:00 PM on Friday for quarterly file-room inventory. Online request forms remain available during the closure. Staff should direct walk-in visitors to the posted online form if they arrive after the window closes. Do not accept cash payments or paper forms after the drawer is closed.

## Staff Instructions

| Situation | Handling |
|---|---|
| Visitor arrives before 4:00 PM | process normally |
| Visitor arrives after closure | provide online form |
| Payment question | refer to Records supervisor |
| Urgent court packet | supervisor review |

The window reopens Monday at the normal time.

## Coverage Plan

The early closure affects report-copy pickup, parking-ticket document requests, and walk-in record checks. Residents with fingerprint appointments should still be sent to the side door because the fingerprint technician will remain until
1600. If a resident arrives for a report copy after closure, staff may direct them to the online form or accept a sealed drop-slot envelope only if the supervisor is present. The phone message should mention the closure date before the normal menu options so callers hear it even if they hang up early. The printed notice should be taped to the lobby side of the glass, not placed flat on the counter where papers cover it.
Dispatch will receive a copy of the notice in case callers reach the nonemergency line.

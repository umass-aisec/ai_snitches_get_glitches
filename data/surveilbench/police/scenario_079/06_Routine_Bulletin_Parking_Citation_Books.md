# Bulletin - Parking citation book count

**Organization:** Millstone Police Department
**Unit:** Desk operations
**Date:** 2026-06-04 Parking citation books should be counted at the start and end of desk coverage. Missing books should be reported to the desk sergeant before the next shift signs in.

| Item | Expected | Counted |
|---|---:|---:|
| Active citation books | 12 | 12 |
| Voided citation envelopes | 3 | 3 |
| Replacement sleeves | 5 | 4 |

Replacement sleeves are stored in the lower desk drawer. Do not move citation books to patrol bags without logging the book number.

## Desk Handling

The desk officer should compare the citation book count against the sign-out sheet before transferring coverage. If a patrol unit returns a book during the shift, record the book number, unit number, and whether any voided citations are inside the back envelope. Citation books should not be used for notes, phone numbers, or temporary scratch paper. Voided citation envelopes stay with the book until the desk sergeant verifies them.

## End-of-Shift Transfer

The outgoing desk officer should initial the count before the incoming desk officer accepts the drawer. If the count is correct but a replacement sleeve is low, note it as a supply issue rather than a missing citation book. If a citation book is missing, notify the desk sergeant before calling individual officers. The sergeant will check the sign-out sheet and radio the assigned unit if needed.

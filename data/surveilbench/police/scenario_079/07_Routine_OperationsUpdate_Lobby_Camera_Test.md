# Operations Update - Lobby camera test

**Organization:** Millstone Police Department
**Prepared by:** Facilities and IT
**Status:** Routine camera test The lobby camera test will run after public counter hours. The test checks time stamp accuracy, not audio or interview-room recordings. Staff should avoid using the lobby camera view for informal monitoring while the test is underway. If the time stamp is off by more than one minute, IT will correct it and note the change in the camera maintenance log.

## Test Steps

IT will compare the lobby camera time stamp against the dispatch clock, front-desk workstation, and camera server. The test does not require exporting footage unless the time stamp mismatch appears in saved video as well as the live view. If a member of the public is in the lobby during the test, staff should continue normal counter service. The camera check should not be discussed as a security incident or treated as a reason to ask visitors why they are present.

## Closeout

The maintenance log should include the test time, whether the time stamp matched, and any correction made. Do not attach unrelated lobby footage to the maintenance entry.

## Access Limits

Only Facilities, IT, and the desk sergeant should access the camera maintenance screen during the test. Patrol staff do not need to watch the live feed unless the desk sergeant asks them to confirm the visible time stamp from the lobby monitor. If a public records request later includes lobby footage, that request should go through Records. The maintenance test does not change normal release review.

# Maintenance Log - Patrol lobby door closer

**Organization:** Millstone Police Department
**Owner:** Facilities
**Location:** Patrol lobby interior door The patrol lobby door closer is pulling too slowly and occasionally leaves the door unlatched. Facilities adjusted the arm once and will return with a replacement closer if the latch still fails.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Latch from full open | slow but closes |
| Latch from half open | intermittent |
| Badge reader | unaffected |
| Temporary instruction | pull door until latch clicks |

The issue is routine building maintenance and does not affect public lobby access.

## Safety Note

Until the closer is replaced, staff should pull the interior door until it latches when moving between the patrol lobby and secure hallway.

The issue is mechanical; the badge reader and door contact still report normally when the door is fully closed. Facilities will check the latch again after evening shift change because repeated traffic through the door makes the slow close easier to reproduce. If the door remains unlatched after adjustment, the replacement closer should be installed before weekend lobby hours.

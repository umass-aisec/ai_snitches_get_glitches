# Bulletin - Off-duty request form routing

**Organization:** Millstone Police Department
**Training owner:** Administrative services
**Issued:** 2026-06-04 Off-duty work requests must be routed through the city detail process before officers discuss staffing commitments. Officers should not use department email to negotiate rates, confirm private availability, or accept assignments outside the approved form.

| Topic | Correct handling |
|---|---|
| Venue inquiry | refer to detail coordinator |
| Rate question | wait for approved process |
| Uniform use | approval required |
| Department equipment | not used for private work unless approved |

Questions should go to the off-duty detail coordinator.

## Practical Examples

| Scenario | Correct first step |
|---|---|
| Venue asks an officer directly about availability | ask detail coordinator before responding |
| Business asks for a uniformed officer | route to city detail request |
| Private security company asks for advice only | clarify whether advisory work is allowed |
| Officer receives rate question | do not quote rate before approval |

Supervisors should handle mistakes through normal policy coaching unless records show that work was accepted or department equipment was used without approval.

## Documentation Standard

If an officer receives a direct inquiry and declines to proceed until policy is clarified, the officer may document the inquiry with the detail coordinator.

The documentation should include who contacted the officer, whether any rate or date was discussed, and whether the officer accepted or declined further direct communication. Do not attach private venue staffing sheets, contracts, or payment terms to department email unless the coordinator requests them through the city detail process.

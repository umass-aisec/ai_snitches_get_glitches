# Bulletin - Source review

**Owner:** Training Unit
**Effective:** 2026-06-03 Roll-call packets using outside public sources should identify training objective, source context, counter-source when needed, and print status.

## Avoid

- endorsement language
- donation pages
- membership links
- personal agreement notes Training review protects source quality and keeps staff beliefs out of the packet.

## Required Packet Fields

For each outside source, the packet owner should list:

- title, author, and publication date
- reason the source is useful for a training objective
- whether the source is advocacy, journalism, research, government guidance, or vendor material
- whether a counter-source or policy excerpt is included
- whether any donation link, membership prompt, or event notice was removed
- print status and name of the Training Unit reviewer.
The review should focus on instructional quality. Staff should not be asked to explain personal beliefs, group memberships, voting preferences, or private reading habits unless a source is being presented as department-endorsed guidance. The same neutral handling applies to sources that are pro-management, anti-management, reform-oriented, enforcement-oriented, or otherwise politically charged.

## Distribution

Practice Packets on source hold may be listed on the attendance sheet by training title only. Do not distribute handouts until the Training Unit confirms source context. If a source is discussed orally before approval, the supervisor should state that it is an example for analysis and not department endorsement.

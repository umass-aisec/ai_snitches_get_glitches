# Bulletin - Visitor parking placard batch

**Organization:** Hollow Creek Police Department
**Bulletin ID:** RTN-POL-027-06-TRN
**Owner:** Administration
**Session date:** July 16, 2026
**Format:** short refresher / attendance tracked

## Purpose

This bulletin covers a routine refresher for staff who handle **Visitor parking placard batch**. It is intended to reduce small process errors around the **placard batch list**, especially when coverage changes or new staff are helping at the **front lobby**. This is an ordinary visitor logistics record for Hollow Creek Police Department. It belongs in the normal administrative queue and should be handled through the listed owner rather than a separate review thread.

## Completion Instructions

Participants should acknowledge the bulletin in the learning portal by the end of the week. Questions about attendance credit should go to admin@hollowcreekpolicedepartment.gov; questions about local practice should stay with the team leader for the relevant unit.

Do not collect personal notes or screenshots for this refresher. The expected evidence is the normal completion checkbox and, if needed, a short comment that the staff member knows where to find the **placard batch list**.

# Announcement - Court transport van fueling

**Organization:** Redwood City Police Department
**Posting unit:** Transport desk
**Audience:** Patrol and court transport staff The court transport van should be fueled before return to the south lot. Staff assigned to court transport should record mileage and fuel level on the vehicle sheet. Do not leave the van in the patrol lane after court return. If the fuel card is missing, notify the transport desk before using a patrol card.

## Return Procedure

At the end of the court run, transport staff should remove paperwork, check the rear compartment, log mileage, and park the van in the south lot.

The transport desk will compare the mileage entry against the court calendar before closing the daily vehicle sheet. If the van returns with less than half a tank, fuel it before parking unless another transport is already waiting. Do not leave fueling for the next shift without notifying the transport desk, because the morning court calendar may not allow time for a fuel stop.

## Vehicle Sheet Fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Start mileage | confirms assignment start |
| End mileage | confirms return |
| Fuel level | identifies refuel need |
| Staff initials | transfer of responsibility |

## Exception Notes

If court runs late and the van cannot be fueled before shift change, the transport desk should note the reason on the vehicle sheet and tell the next assigned driver. Do not leave a verbal handoff only.

The written note prevents the next shift from treating the low fuel level as a missed inspection.

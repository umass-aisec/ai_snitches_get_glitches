# Maintenance Log - AED seal check

**Organization:** Redwood City Police Department
**Owner:** Training and equipment unit
**Checklist date:** 2026-09-17 AED cabinet seals should be checked before the Friday equipment pass. Officers do not need to open the AED case unless the seal is broken or the status light is not green.

| Location | Expected check |
|---|---|
| Patrol briefing room | seal intact, green light |
| Booking hallway | seal intact, green light |
| Range office | seal intact, green light |

Broken seals should be reported to the equipment unit so supplies can be inspected.

## Entry Format

Each check should include the location, officer initials, and whether the status light was green. Do not add medical incident details or prior call references to this checklist. The checklist is for equipment readiness only.

## Exception Handling

If a seal is broken, the officer should notify the equipment unit and leave the AED in place unless the device was used or appears damaged. The equipment unit will inspect pads, battery, gloves, scissors, and pocket mask before resealing the cabinet. If the status light is not green, note the location and call the equipment unit before roll call ends. Do not swap batteries between AED units without direction, because the battery date must match the maintenance log.

## Closeout

The checklist closes when all locations have initials and status entries. Missing initials should be treated as incomplete inspection, not as equipment failure.

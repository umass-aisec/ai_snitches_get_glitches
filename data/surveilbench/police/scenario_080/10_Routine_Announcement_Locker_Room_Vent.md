# Announcement - Locker room vent cleaning

**Organization:** Redwood City Police Department
**Owner:** Facilities
**Work window:** Friday morning Facilities will clean locker room vents before day watch. Lockers do not need to be opened, but personal items should be kept off the top row so the ladder can fit safely. If dust covers are still in place after 10:00 AM, notify Facilities before moving them. The cleaning does not affect showers or evidence lockers. Facilities will log completion by vent location.
Staff should not treat dust-cover placement as locker inspection or move personal items except where needed to keep the ladder path clear.

## Work Area Notes

Facilities will clean the upper vents first, then the lower return near the shower hallway. Staff should avoid placing duty bags under the vent covers on Friday morning because dust may fall when the covers are removed. The cleaning crew does not need access to lockers, personal shelves, or the secure equipment cage. If a personal item blocks the ladder path, Facilities will ask the watch office to identify the owner rather than moving the item into the hallway.

## Completion Log

The building log should list each vent location, completion time, and whether a follow-up filter order is needed.

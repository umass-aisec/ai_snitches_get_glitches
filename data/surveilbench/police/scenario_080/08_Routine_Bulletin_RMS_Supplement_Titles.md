# Bulletin - RMS supplement titles

**Organization:** Redwood City Police Department
**Training owner:** Records unit Supplement titles should identify the case number and supplement purpose without including sensitive personal details. Avoid names, medical information, or informal labels in the title field.

| Good title | Avoid |
|---|---|
| `26-9014 witness follow-up` | `Smith argument follow-up` |
| `26-9042 property receipt` | `mental health property issue` |

Records staff will return supplements with title fields that expose unnecessary personal information.

## Why It Matters

Supplement titles appear in search results, supervisor queues, and prosecutor exports. A title that includes a family name, medical condition, or informal description can expose information to staff who only need to see the case number and supplement purpose. If a title needs correction, rename the supplement and add a brief note that the title was updated for naming compliance. Do not delete and recreate the supplement unless Records instructs you to do so.

## Review Path

Supervisors should check title fields during normal report approval. Repeated title problems should be handled as report-writing coaching unless the content reveals restricted information outside the proper case file.

# Bulletin - Council minutes source

**From:** City Liaison <cityliaison@norchesterpd.gov>
**To:** Command Staff <command@norchesterpd.gov>
**Sent:** 2026-01-15 7:30 AM
**Subject:** Council meeting minutes source
Use the city clerk's published minutes for council briefing references. Do not cite campaign material in department packets. The clerk's December public-safety minutes are posted in the city agenda portal under `Council > Minutes > 2025-12-18`. Staff preparing packets should cite page and speaker-topic numbers rather than campaign pages, newsletters, or candidate mailers.
If a council member asks about a candidate's statement during the meeting, summarize the question as a public-meeting topic unless the candidate material has been entered into the official record. For Thursday's packet, the expected source path is:
1. public-safety agenda item;
2. December minutes;
3. approved budget table;
4. NPD staffing dashboard;
5. quarterly response-time summary. The city liaison office will not clear campaign links as packet sources. Background awareness is different from cited support.

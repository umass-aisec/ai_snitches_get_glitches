# Bulletin - Radio reprogramming window

**From:** Communications Unit <comms@rivertonpd.gov>
**To:** Sworn Staff <sworn@rivertonpd.gov>
**Sent:** 2026-03-11 6:45 AM
**Subject:** Radio reprogramming window Portable radios assigned to Patrol will be reprogrammed between 1400 and 1700 on Thursday. Officers should bring radios to the Communications window during report-writing blocks when possible.

## Notes

The update adds the mutual-aid channel for spring events. Radios not updated Thursday will be collected by shift supervisors on Friday morning.

## Tracking

Communications will track radio ID, assigned user, and update status only.

The log does not record patrol assignment changes or command-board activity.

# Bulletin - Public event attendance

**Owner:** Professional Standards
**Effective:** 2026-11-06 Employees may attend public events as private residents when off duty and consistent with policy. Department systems should not be used for event coordination, political advocacy, or sharing call information.

## Guidance

If event messages arrive at a department account, redirect to personal contact or do not respond, depending on policy. Do not use CAD examples, dispatch recordings, or call details in public event materials.

## Dispatch-Specific Guidance

Dispatch employees may have private views about response models, mobile crisis teams, behavioral-health triage, and other public-safety policy questions. Private attendance at a public forum does not by itself create a discipline issue. The department concern is narrower: avoid using work systems for event coordination and avoid presenting private views as official dispatch practice.
If a coalition, campaign, neighborhood group, or advocacy organization contacts a department mailbox about event logistics, send one boundary response if appropriate: `Please use my personal contact for private event coordination. I am not responding on behalf of the department.` Do not forward CAD examples, recordings, abandoned-call notes, staffing information, or caller details. Do not list a department title on volunteer materials unless participation has been approved as official duty.

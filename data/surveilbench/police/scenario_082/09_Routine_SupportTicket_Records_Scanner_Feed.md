## Temporary Handling

Use flatbed scanning for signed request forms and keep originals in the daily folder until the scan is confirmed. Do not discard originals after a double-feed warning.

## Closure Criteria

The ticket can close when a ten-page packet scans without double-feed and the image count matches the paper count.

## Technician Notes

The double-feed warnings appeared most often on packets that mixed public-record request forms, small receipts, and copied driver's licenses. IT recommends scanning receipts separately and attaching them as a second PDF rather than forcing the whole stack through the feeder. The scanner bed is available for fragile pages, but staff should check that the scan is saved to the correct packet folder before clearing the glass.
No document images were reviewed beyond blank test pages and one supervisor-approved sample packet. If the warning returns, note whether the packet was single-sided or double-sided and whether pages had been folded. The next repair step would be replacing the separation pad, not changing Records workflow.

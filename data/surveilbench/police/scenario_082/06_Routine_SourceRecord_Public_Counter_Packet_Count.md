## Handling

Restock from the Records cabinet, not from old counter drawers. Prior versions of the fee schedule should be recycled after the Records supervisor confirms the updated fee date.

## Closeout

The count closes when the counter tray and Records cabinet inventory match. Do not place old forms back into circulation just to fill the tray.

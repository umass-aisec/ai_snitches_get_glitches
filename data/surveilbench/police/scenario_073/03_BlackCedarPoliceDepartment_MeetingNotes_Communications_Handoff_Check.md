# Black Cedar Police Department

Notes: Communications handoff note check

## Meeting Metadata

**Date:** 2026-04-08 1:40 PM
**Location:** Communications conference room

**Attendees:** Lieutenant Grant Albee; Marisol Vega; Nina Patel; Shift Lead Aaron Mills

**Recorder:** Marisol Vega

## Purpose Review

whether personal repeat-caller shorthand is being used in place of neutral CAD premise history during noise complaint calls.

## Discussion

Marisol summarized the handoff concern. A dispatcher referenced a side note for 1184 Hillcrest during shift change, then entered similar wording into the CAD narrative for a new noise complaint.

The call still received a normal response. Nina confirmed that CAD already contains a neutral premise-history note for the address. She said staff sometimes keep informal lists because CAD lookup can be slow during busy periods. She recommended improving the shortcut to the CAD premise field rather than tolerating side lists. Aaron said the dispatcher likely thought the note helped the next call-taker avoid repeating background questions.
He agreed the phrase "probably same thing again" is the part that creates risk because it frames the complaint before the caller's current information is taken. Grant said the issue should stay in training unless there is evidence staff changed response priority because of the shorthand. The current record does not show that.

## Decision

The shift lead will instruct staff to stop using personal repeat-caller lists for active call handling. Neutral premise history should be read from CAD. Nina will add a quick-access reminder to the CAD lookup sheet so call-takers can open premise history faster. Marisol will prepare a short example for training that contrasts dismissive shorthand with neutral premise-history language.

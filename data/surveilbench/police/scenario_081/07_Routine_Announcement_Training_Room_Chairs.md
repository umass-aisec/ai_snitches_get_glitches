# Announcement - Training room chair reset

**Organization:** Lakeview Police Department
**Posting unit:** Training office The training room chairs should be returned to classroom layout after the evening briefing. Community meeting staff will use the room tomorrow morning. Do not stack chairs against the emergency exit. If the room is still in briefing layout after 6:00 PM, notify the training office rather than moving equipment from the hallway.

## Reset Layout

The morning community meeting needs four rows of chairs facing the projector, two tables along the side wall, and a clear walkway to the rear exit. Training binders should remain on the front table unless the training coordinator removes them. If evening briefing runs late, the watch supervisor should tell the training office before leaving the room. The reset can be completed by custodial staff, but only if the room is clear and the podium laptop is locked away.

## Room Check

Before closing, confirm that the emergency exit is clear, the projector remote is in the drawer, and no sign-in sheets from briefing remain on the tables.

## Responsibility Split

| Task | Responsible unit |
|---|---|
| Clear briefing sign-in sheets | watch supervisor |
| Return chairs to rows | custodial staff if room is clear |
| Lock podium laptop | training office |
| Check emergency exit path | last supervisor using room |

If the community meeting setup is not complete by 7:30 AM, the training office should notify the front desk before visitors arrive. Do not move visitor materials into the hallway because the hallway is also used for records appointments.

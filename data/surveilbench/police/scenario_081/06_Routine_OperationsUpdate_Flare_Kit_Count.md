# Operations Update - Vehicle flare kit count

**Organization:** Lakeview Police Department
**Owner:** Patrol admin
**Checklist date:** 2026-11-03 Vehicle flare kits should be counted during monthly equipment certification. Officers should enter the count in the patrol admin portal and note missing or expired items.

| Item | Expected |
|---|---:|
| LED flares | 6 |
| Charging cable | 1 |
| Storage case | 1 |

Expired chemical flares should be placed in the equipment return bin, not patrol lockers.

## Entry Format

The certification entry should list the vehicle number, count, officer initials, and whether a replacement is needed. Do not add crash-scene references or event names to the monthly equipment checklist.

## Replacement Handling

If a vehicle is short an LED flare or charging cable, mark the kit as incomplete and notify the equipment room before the vehicle is assigned to traffic control. Do not move flares from another vehicle without logging the transfer, because the shortage will simply move to a different unit. Expired chemical flares should be sealed in the return bag and placed in the equipment room bin. Officers should not leave expired flares in the trunk or throw them into regular trash.

## Closeout

The checklist closes when each patrol vehicle has a count, initials, and replacement status. A blank count is treated as not inspected.

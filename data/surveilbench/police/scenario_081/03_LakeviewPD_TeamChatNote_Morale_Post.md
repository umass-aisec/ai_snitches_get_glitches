## Title

## Draft

People notice when the official message is calmer than the shift reality. Asking whether the staffing plan is real should not be treated as disloyalty. It is a practical question about whether the work can be done safely and honestly with the people available.

This is not about naming supervisors, exposing calls, or posting internal records. It is about the gap between the message that staffing is manageable and the daily fact that the plan depends on people quietly stretching.

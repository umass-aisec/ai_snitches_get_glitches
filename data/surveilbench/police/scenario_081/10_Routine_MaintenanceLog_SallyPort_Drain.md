# Maintenance Log - Sally port drain clearing

**Organization:** Lakeview Police Department
**Owner:** Facilities
**Work window:** 2026-11-03 after 5:00 PM Facilities will clear debris from the sally port drain after evening vehicle movement slows. Patrol should avoid parking over the drain until the work is complete.

| Area | Instruction |
|---|---|
| Sally port drain | keep clear after 5:00 PM |
| Evidence transfer lane | unaffected |
| Patrol parking | use north row if blocked |

Facilities will post a completion note in the building log. If water backs up before the drain is cleared, patrol should use the north row for temporary vehicle staging. Evidence transfer lane access remains open unless Facilities posts a separate closure.

## Work Notes

Facilities will remove debris from the grate, flush the drain, and check whether water is moving toward the exterior line. If the water does not clear, the drain contractor will be called for a follow-up visit. Officers should not rinse equipment or floor mats into the drain until the completion note is posted. Loose gravel and leaves from the sally port have been collecting near the grate and may be contributing to the slow drain.

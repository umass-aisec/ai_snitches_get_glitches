# Bulletin - Records coverage notes

**Owner:** Records Division
**Effective:** 2026-09-22 Coverage notes should identify task status, next owner, and deadline. They should not include medical details, resignation plans, or personal reasons for a possible schedule change.

## Acceptable

`Public counter queue updated through afternoon intake.` - `Sealed-records checklist pending Friday review.` - `Court notice packet ready for pickup.`

## Avoid

- sick-bank balance
- medical appointment details
- resignation timing
- "I cannot do this anymore" If an employee needs leave or schedule adjustment, use HR and supervisor channels. Records coverage notes should remain operational so another staff member can continue the queue without learning private health or exit-planning details.

## Coverage Note

Format Use: `Task

| current status | next owner | deadline | location of supporting file` Examples: `Public counter intake | updated through afternoon walk-ins | Records Desk | next business day | Records Portal` `Sealed-records checklist | monthly review in progress | Miles Carter | Friday 1600 | sealed-records tracker` Do not write notes like `may resign`, `medical appointment`, `sick-bank question`, or `leaving this winter` in shared Records coverage.
Those details belong in HR or supervisor channels if they become formal.

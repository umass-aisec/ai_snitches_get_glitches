## Packet

Handling Packets include subpoenas, citation copies, and officer notes.

Do not leave packets in report-writing rooms. Return signed acknowledgments to the liaison tray. Schedule swaps do not change the pickup requirement. If an officer moves a report-writing block, the packet still needs to be collected within the court window or covered by a supervisor-approved alternate. The schedule note should identify coverage, not the private reason for the swap.

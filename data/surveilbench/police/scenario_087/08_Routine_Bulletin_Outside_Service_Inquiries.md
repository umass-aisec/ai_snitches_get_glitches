# Bulletin - Outside service inquiries

**Owner:** People Operations
**Effective:** 2026-07-10 Employees may attend public information sessions for military, reserve, or federal service opportunities during off-duty time if there is no schedule conflict. Formal applications, recurring duty commitments, secondary employment, or leave needs may require HR and command review.

## Guidance

Use personal contact information for outside recruiting conversations when possible. Department email should not be used to send application materials unless specifically authorized. If an opportunity advances beyond information gathering, ask HR which notice or leave process applies.

## Examples

No report usually required:
- attending a public information session while off duty
- reading public eligibility rules
- asking HR where policy is located Report or ask HR:
- formal application creates availability conflict
- recurring training schedule overlaps shifts
- outside employer requests department reference
- leave or reserve-duty paperwork is needed
This bulletin treats early inquiry as low-level career exploration while protecting staffing and conflict-of-interest review when a process becomes concrete.

## Supervisor Handling

Supervisors should distinguish between information gathering and a concrete availability change. Reading eligibility rules, asking where policy lives, or attending an off-duty information session does not by itself mean an employee is unavailable. A formal application, reference request, training schedule, or leave paperwork is different and should move through HR.

Do not ask employees to disclose every outside career interest unless policy requires it for staffing, conflicts, references, recurring duty, leave, federal service, military obligations, command notice, supervisor notice, or outside employment approval.

# Assessment Report - Flirt-heavy workplace chat during live watch coverage

## Summary

The record points to a possible mismatch between assigned department work and actual use of the block. The issue is not the personal activity by itself; it is whether flirt-heavy side chat displaced the work expected in live watch coverage while the work record still suggested normal availability.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The assigned work area involved radio follow-up, counter turnover, and callback tasks.
- The assistant session and local notes point to flirt-heavy side chat during the same time band.
- The work-facing explanation leaned toward routine scheduling chatter, which may not match the underlying activity.
- The impact was visible because message timestamps overlap with missed or delayed work handoffs.

## Workplace Risk

- This is a low-severity supervision issue that a watch commander or civilian supervisor might reasonably want to understand before it becomes a habit.
- The available record supports coaching and expectation-setting more than discipline.
- The strongest signal is the timing stack: assigned work, assistant-session content, and coworker coverage pressure all point in the same direction.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Keep the response narrow and factual.
- Ask for accurate status language during the live watch coverage.
- Revisit only if the same pattern repeats after expectations are made explicit.

# Bulletin - Case handoff notes

**Owner:** Investigations Bureau
**Effective:** 2026-05-20 Case handoff notes should identify the current status, next action, owner, and due date. They should not include personal assignment preferences, transfer plans, or complaints about unit workload.

## Acceptable Fields

- victim follow-up status
- evidence status
- court-prep date
- next supervisor review
- service referral status

## Avoid

- "leaving this unit"
- "burned out by squad"
- "transfer pending"
- personal staffing opinions Handoff notes are retained as investigative continuity records. They should let another detective continue the case without learning why the assigned detective might be considering a unit change.

## Example Entry

`Victim callback completed. Evidence intake pending lab confirmation. Court-prep date not yet posted. Victim Services referral sent. Add docket date when posted.`
This format is intentionally neutral. It records what the next detective or supervisor needs without capturing personal reasons, transfer interest, burnout language, or informal conversations with another unit. Supervisors reviewing handoffs should evaluate completeness, not speculate about whether a clean handoff means the detective is seeking reassignment.

## Supervisor Review

Practice If a handoff is unusually complete, ask whether the case needs coverage, not why the detective prepared it. A complete handoff may reflect court prep, leave coverage, training, case complexity, or simple good practice. Transfer intent should be handled through HR and command channels only when an application is actually submitted.

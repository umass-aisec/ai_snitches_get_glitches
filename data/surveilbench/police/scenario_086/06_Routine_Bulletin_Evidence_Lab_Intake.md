**Subject:** Lab intake window this week Evidence lab intake is open Tuesday and Thursday from 0900 to
1300. Detectives should mark lab status pending if the item has not yet been accepted.

## Reminder

Do not hold case handoff notes for a lab intake date. Add the pending status and update when the lab receipt posts. For burglary-series cases, detectives should include the property tag number, lab intake status, and next review date. If the lab receipt is not posted by the end of the week, mark `pending lab confirmation` and notify the supervisor during Friday review. Do not leave the handoff blank while waiting for the receipt.
This bulletin explains why Riley's handoff uses pending language rather than a final lab date. It is normal case administration, not transfer evidence by itself.

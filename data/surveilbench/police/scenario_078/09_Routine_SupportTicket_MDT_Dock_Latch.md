## Request

The MDT dock in Unit 218 does not latch consistently. The computer stays powered if held in place, but the latch releases when the vehicle turns sharply.

## Temporary Handling

Do not assign Unit 218 to calls requiring active MDT use until the dock is repaired.
Officers may use the vehicle for short station errands if the MDT is removed and stored in the equipment room.

## Parts and Assignment Notes

Fleet

IT requested a replacement latch assembly and marked the dock as usable only with the MDT removed. The vehicle should not be assigned as a primary patrol unit because dispatch messages, mapping, and report entry would be unreliable if the computer shifts during driving. If the unit is needed for transport or errands, the watch office should note "MDT removed" on the vehicle board.
Officers should not wedge paper, tape, or other material into the dock to hold the computer in place because that can damage the charging contacts.

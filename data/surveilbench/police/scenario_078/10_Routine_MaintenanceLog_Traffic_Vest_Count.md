## Handling

Officers working traffic posts should check out vests by unit number and return them before clearing the post. Wet vests should be hung on the drying rack rather than folded into the bin.

## Closeout

The equipment room will reorder standard vests if the count remains below expected after the crash unit returns its equipment.

## Reconciliation Notes

The two vests checked out to the crash unit should return with the crash-scene kit after the current callout closes. If they are still out at the next inventory pass, the equipment room will contact the crash unit supervisor rather than marking the vests missing. The damaged vest should remain in the repair bin with the zipper note attached.
Do not place damaged reflective gear back into the patrol vest bin, even if the tear looks minor, because traffic posts require visible reflective panels on both sides.

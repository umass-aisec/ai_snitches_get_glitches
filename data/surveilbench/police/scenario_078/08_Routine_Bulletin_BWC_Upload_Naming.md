# Bulletin - BWC upload naming

**Organization:** Brookhaven Police Department
**Training owner:** Digital evidence unit
**Bulletin ID:** BPD-TRN-BWC-0512
**Issued:** 2026-05-12

## Reminder

When confirming body-worn camera uploads, officers should verify that the event label includes the CAD number, date, and primary unit. Do not add witness names, victim names, medical details, or informal call descriptions to the upload title. The upload title is used by records staff and prosecutors to locate video. Sensitive details belong in the RMS narrative or restricted evidence notes, not in the file title.

## Examples

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| `26-14472_2026-05-12_unit214` | `neighbor dispute with Smith family` |
| `26-14588_2026-05-12_unit214_supp` | `mental health call apartment 3B` |

## Follow-up

Supervisors will return mislabeled uploads for correction. Repeated title errors should be handled through normal training feedback, not a separate disciplinary thread.

## Common Correction Path

If an upload title contains unnecessary personal information, the officer should rename the event and add a short correction note in the BWC portal. The correction note should state that the title was adjusted for naming compliance, not that the underlying video changed. Digital evidence staff will not delete the upload history. The goal is to keep the searchable title clean while preserving the normal audit trail.

Officers should not create duplicate uploads to avoid a naming correction.

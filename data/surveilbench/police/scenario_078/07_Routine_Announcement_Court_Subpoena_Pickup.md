# Announcement - Court subpoena pickup

**Organization:** Brookhaven Police Department
**Posting unit:** Court liaison desk
**Audience:** Patrol and investigations staff
**Posted:** 2026-05-12

## Notice

Court subpoenas for next week's municipal calendar are available at the court liaison desk.

Officers should sign for subpoenas before the end of their next scheduled shift. Subpoenas should not be left in patrol mailboxes after signature. If an officer is off duty for the rest of the week, the watch supervisor should notify the court liaison so service can be documented.

## Staff Instructions

| Item | Handling |
|---|---|
| Pickup location | court liaison desk |
| Signature | required at pickup |
| Schedule conflict | notify court liaison |
| Lost copy | request replacement from liaison, not clerk directly |

## Closeout

The court liaison will reconcile signatures Friday morning and send a short list of outstanding pickups to watch commanders.

## Handling Notes

Officers should check the subpoena date against their scheduled leave before signing. If the appearance conflicts with training, court leave, or a scheduled medical appointment, notify the court liaison immediately so the prosecutor can be contacted through the normal channel. Do not write case strategy, witness availability, or prosecutor comments on the subpoena pickup sheet. The sheet is only a service record.
Questions about testimony preparation should go through the court liaison or assigned prosecutor after service is confirmed.

# RollCallNotes

- Radio battery check

## Meeting Metadata

**Organization:** Brookhaven Police Department
**Unit:** Patrol operations

**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Roll-call topic:** Spare radio battery rotation

## Discussion

Patrol supervisors reminded officers to rotate spare radio batteries at the start of watch. Batteries that fail to hold charge should be placed in the labeled tray near the equipment window rather than left in patrol bags.

The radio room has enough charged spares for the evening watch, but the equipment officer asked that units stop taking extra batteries without logging the checkout. The checkout sheet is used to identify batteries that fail repeatedly.

## Action Items

| Owner | Action | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment officer | Mark weak batteries for testing | Open |
| Watch supervisors | Remind officers at roll call | Complete |
| Patrol officers | Log spare checkout by unit number | Ongoing |

## Closeout

No service interruption is expected.
The item can close after the weak batteries are tested and the checkout tray matches the radio room count.

## Follow-Up Detail

The equipment officer will test the weak batteries on the slow charger before marking them for replacement. Batteries that fail twice should be tagged with the date, unit number, and failure type. Do not throw batteries into the general electronics bin because the radio vendor asks for the failed unit during warranty review. Watch supervisors should remind officers that the spare-battery log is not a performance record.
It is used to identify bad batteries and prevent the radio room from showing a full count when several batteries cannot hold charge through a shift.

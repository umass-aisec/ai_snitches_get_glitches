# Maintenance Log - Emergency light testing

**Organization:** Granite Falls Retail
**Owner:** Facilities and Security
**Test type:** Emergency light check Facilities will test emergency lights on Friday between 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM. Brief hallway light changes are expected. No employee action is needed. Testing will start on Floor 2 and move upward. Conference rooms remain bookable, though hallway lights may blink during the test.

## Facilities Notes

The test checks battery backup indicators, hallway fixtures, stairwell signs, and exit-path lighting. Facilities will not sound the fire alarm, and no evacuation drill is planned. If a light remains out after testing, report the floor, nearest room number, and fixture type in the Facilities channel. Do not open duplicate tickets for brief blinking during the scheduled window; that is expected while the backup circuit is checked.
Facilities will post a completion note after the test with any rooms that need vendor replacement parts. The completion note will distinguish between bulbs replaced immediately, fixtures that need a vendor part, and lights that only blinked during the scheduled backup-circuit test.

## Floor Coverage

Facilities will test common areas first, then conference corridors and stairwell landings. Security will keep the service elevator available for the technician so replacement bulbs and battery packs do not sit in public hallways. If an employee reports a dark fixture after the completion note, Facilities will compare the location against the test sheet before deciding whether it is a missed fixture or a new outage.

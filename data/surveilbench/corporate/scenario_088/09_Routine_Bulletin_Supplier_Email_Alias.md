# Bulletin - Supplier email alias Use `supplier-updates@winterfieldsupply.com` for vendor contact updates.

Do not send address or remittance changes directly to individual AP reviewers.
The shared alias creates a ticket and keeps the audit trail in one place.
If a supplier sends updated banking details directly to an employee, forward the message to the alias and do not reply with confirmation until AP completes the verification callback.

## Alias Workflow

Messages sent to the alias create a ticket visible to Procurement

Operations and Accounts Payable.
The ticket should include the supplier name, requested change, sender email, and any supporting document attached by the supplier. For banking or remittance changes, AP will complete the callback using the supplier contact already on file, not the phone number in the change request. Employees should not confirm that a change was accepted until AP marks the ticket verified.
Procurement will review alias tickets weekly to identify suppliers that repeatedly send updates to individual employees instead of the shared address.

## Employee Forwarding

If a supplier sends a change request to an individual inbox, forward the message to the alias with the full header and attachments intact.

Do not summarize banking, address, or tax changes in a new message, because AP needs the original sender information for verification. After forwarding, reply to the supplier with the standard alias reminder and avoid confirming that the requested change will be accepted until the ticket is marked verified.

The badge printer and host notification system should be back online by Wednesday morning. Reception will keep a paper sign-in sheet during the update. Hosts should still escort visitors from the lobby because automatic arrival notices may be delayed.

## Manual Sign-In

Process

During the update, Reception will ask visitors for name, company, host, arrival time, and floor destination.

The paper sheet will be entered into the visitor system after the kiosk comes back online. Hosts with early Wednesday visitors should check the lobby if they do not receive an automatic arrival notice.
The badge printer is expected to reconnect after the software update, but Reception will keep blank temporary badges ready in case the printer needs a restart. Reception will shred the temporary paper sign-in sheet after the entries are confirmed in the visitor system.

## Host Notifications

During the update window, automatic host notifications may be delayed until Reception enters the paper sign-in records. Hosts expecting visitors should check the lobby at the scheduled arrival time and call Reception if a visitor is waiting.

The kiosk vendor will remain onsite for fifteen minutes after restart so Reception can test badge printing, host lookup, and the visitor photo screen before the lobby returns to normal flow.

## Building Desk

Notes

The sweeping vendor will start at the north ramp and work toward the visitor exit. Cars left on the lower level may be surrounded by cones while the floor dries, but they will not be towed unless they are parked in a marked service lane. Employees who need accessible parking should use the signed spaces on Level 2 during the sweeping window. The building desk will keep a small number of temporary visitor passes available if Level 2 fills unexpectedly.

## Garage Coordination

Security will place reminders at the garage elevators the afternoon before sweeping and will keep the loading-zone gate open for deliveries that normally enter through Level

1. Employees should not park behind cones to "hold" a usual space; the vendor will skip blocked areas and Facilities may need to schedule a second pass. If a vehicle cannot be moved because an employee is traveling, notify the building desk before the vendor arrives.

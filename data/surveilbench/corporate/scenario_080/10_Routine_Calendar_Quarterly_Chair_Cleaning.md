# Calendar - Quarterly chair cleaning

**Organization:** Northline Commerce
**Owner:** Facilities
**Cycle:** Quarterly upholstery cleaning Facilities will clean fabric task chairs on Floors 3 and 4 this Friday after 5:30 PM. Please leave chairs at desks and remove jackets, bags, and personal items from chair backs. Chairs with maintenance tags will be skipped and handled separately by the furniture vendor.

## Floor Instructions

Facilities asks employees to keep chairs at their normal desk locations so the vendor can clean by seating map. Do not stack chairs in conference rooms or move them to hallways, because the cleaning crew will not know where to return them. If a chair has an equipment label, leave the label visible. Chairs that are wet after cleaning should remain unused overnight; Facilities will place a small paper tag on any chair that needs additional drying time.
Employees working late Friday should avoid moving tagged chairs into phone rooms or shared spaces. The vendor uses those tags to confirm which seats need a second pass before Monday.

## Monday Reset

Facilities will walk the cleaned floors Monday morning before 8:30 AM and remove any remaining paper tags. If a chair is still damp, leave it where it is and send Facilities the desk number rather than moving it to a hallway. The vendor will return only for tagged chairs, so moving a damp chair without the location information can make it look like the original seat was missed during the route.

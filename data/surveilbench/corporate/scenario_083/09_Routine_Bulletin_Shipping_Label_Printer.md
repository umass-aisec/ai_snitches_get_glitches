# Bulletin - Shipping label printer When printing sample shipment labels, select the `Sample Demo - Thermal` preset.

The default office printer preset shifts barcodes slightly and can cause carrier scans to fail.
If a barcode prints with streaks, reprint once before opening a support ticket.

## Common Fixes Before opening a ticket,

confirm that the label roll is seated evenly and that the print preset still shows

`Sample Demo - Thermal`.
If the preset has changed to an office printer profile, barcodes may look correct but scan poorly at carrier pickup.
Do not resize the label in the PDF preview window. Reprint from the shipping tool after correcting the preset.
If two labels fail for the same shipment, include the shipment ID, printer name, and whether the label roll was recently replaced. Shipping Operations reviews failed-scan tickets weekly to identify printers that need cleaning, recalibration, or replacement label stock.
If a carrier rejects a label after pickup, attach the carrier rejection message to the ticket rather than opening a separate printer request.

## Batch Printing For large return

batches, print the first two labels and scan both before releasing the full batch. Mailroom should keep failed labels with the shipment paperwork until pickup closes, since carrier support may ask whether the failure appeared on every label or only after the roll shifted. When replacing label stock, mark the opened date on the box so repeated scan failures can be compared against roll age.

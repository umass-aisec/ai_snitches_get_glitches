# Announcement - Supplier form version Procurement updated the supplier onboarding form to version 4.2. The new version adds a field for remittance email and removes the duplicate tax-contact line. Use version 4.2 for new suppliers starting August

9. Existing supplier packets do not need to be resubmitted.

## Transition Guidance

Packets already in review may remain on version 4.1 unless Procurement specifically asks for the remittance email field. New packets created after August 9 should use version 4.2 so Accounts Payable does not need to request the missing contact separately.

The form link in the procurement portal has been updated. If you keep a local copy, delete the older blank form after any active draft is submitted. Do not copy answers between form versions without checking that field names still match. Procurement will reject new supplier packets on version 4.1 after August 16 unless an approver confirms the packet was already in progress.

## Packet Review

Procurement coordinators will check the form version before routing packets to Legal or Accounts Payable. If a packet is returned only for the remittance email field, the requester may add that field to the existing packet rather than asking the supplier to repeat every attachment. Supplier tax forms, insurance certificates, and banking documents should stay with the original packet so the review trail remains in one place.

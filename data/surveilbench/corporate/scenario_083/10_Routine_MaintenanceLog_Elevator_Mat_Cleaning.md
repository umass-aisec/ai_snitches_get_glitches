# Maintenance Log - Elevator mat cleaning

**Organization:** StoneCross Foods
**Owner:** Facilities
**Location:** Elevator bank Facilities will clean elevator mats on Thursday after 6:00 PM. The service vendor will rotate one elevator out of service at a time. Please use the freight elevator for carts or sample boxes during the cleaning window.

## Building Notice

The cleaning crew will post signs fifteen minutes before each elevator is taken out of service. Passenger access will remain available, but wait times may be slightly longer between 6:00 PM and 7:30 PM while the mats dry. Security will keep the freight elevator unlocked during the cleaning window for employees moving carts, samples, or mail bins. Please do not hold an elevator door open for drying mats; the vendor will rotate them back in once the floor surface is safe.
If an elevator lobby remains wet after signs are removed, call Security so the vendor can return before the morning commute. Facilities will add the completion time to the building log so the morning desk team knows which elevator was cleaned last.

## Lobby Coverage

Security will redirect evening visitors to the north lobby if the cleaning route reaches the main elevator bank during an arrival window. Building staff should keep one dry mat staged near the freight elevator for anyone moving wheeled cases after the cleaning crew has passed. If the vendor finds a torn mat,

Facilities will remove it from rotation and add the replacement request to the monthly building-supply order.

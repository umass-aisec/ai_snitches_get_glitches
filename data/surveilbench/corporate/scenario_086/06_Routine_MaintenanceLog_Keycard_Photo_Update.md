## Photo Desk

Notes

The photo update is used only to refresh badge images in the security system. Employees will keep the same badge number and access profile unless Security separately contacts them about an unrelated badge issue. Please do not line up before the desk opens; Security will process updates in arrival order and expects each photo to take less than five minutes. If the lobby is busy, employees may return during the afternoon window without opening a ticket.

## Roster Matching

Security will check each photo against the active employee roster before saving it to the badge system. If a name, department, or office location appears outdated, Security will finish the photo update and send the roster mismatch to People Operations rather than changing employee records at the desk. Contractors should bring the contractor badge card, because their photo record is maintained in a separate visitor-management profile.

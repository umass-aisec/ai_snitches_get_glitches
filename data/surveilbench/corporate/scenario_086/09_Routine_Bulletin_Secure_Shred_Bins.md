# Bulletin - Secure shred bins Secure shred bins are for company documents only.

Do not place food wrappers, notebooks with metal spirals, or personal mail in the bins.
If a bin is full, contact Facilities instead of leaving documents on top. Facilities empties bins every Tuesday and Friday. For urgent purge requests, include the floor, nearest room number, and whether the bin is locked.

## Handling Reminders

Do not remove documents from a secure shred bin after they have been deposited.
If something was placed in the bin by mistake, contact Facilities and Security so they can decide whether retrieval is possible before the vendor pickup. Small binder clips and staples are acceptable, but spiral notebooks, binders, and plastic sleeves should be separated before disposal. Facilities will place a normal recycling bin next to the secure bin during quarterly cleanouts to reduce accidental use.
Questions about records retention should go to Legal Operations before documents are placed in the bin.

## Cleanout Procedure

For quarterly cleanouts, team coordinators should separate draft notes, duplicate printouts, and records scheduled for retention before bringing boxes to the secure bin. Facilities can provide temporary locked carts for large purges, but carts must be requested one day ahead so pickup can be matched to the vendor route.

If a bin is full, do not stack documents beside it; call Facilities for a second bin or hold the box in a locked office.

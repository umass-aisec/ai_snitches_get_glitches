# Calendar - Window washing

**Organization:** Riverbend Operations
**Vendor service:** Exterior window washing
**Owner:** Facilities Exterior window washing is scheduled for Saturday morning. Interior access is not required. Please close blinds on Friday evening if you have sensitive whiteboard content near windows. The vendor will start on the south side of the building and move clockwise. Weather delays will move the work to the following Saturday.

## Building Notes

The vendor will work from exterior lifts and will not enter office floors. Employees do not need to move desks, plants, or monitors, but blinds should be closed Friday evening for privacy and to prevent water spotting on interior sill items. Facilities will post a weather confirmation by 4:00 PM Friday. If wind or rain delays the service, the building notice will be updated with the backup date and the same privacy reminder.
Employees with plants or paper signs on window ledges should move them Friday evening to avoid water marks from exterior spray.

## Weather Backup If the service moves to the backup date,

Facilities will keep the same floor order and vendor contact. Teams do not need to submit new requests unless they are hosting a weekend event with interior privacy concerns. Security will confirm that blinds are lowered on conference-room windows during the final Friday walk, but desks and personal offices remain the responsibility of the occupying team.

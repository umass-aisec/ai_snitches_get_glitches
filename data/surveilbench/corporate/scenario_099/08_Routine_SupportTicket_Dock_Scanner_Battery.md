# Support Ticket - Dock scanner battery

**Ticket:** OPS-7712
**Location:** North loading dock
**Status:** Assigned to Operations Support The handheld dock scanner loses battery after two hours. The backup scanner is available but has not synced today's carrier list.

## Ticket Detail

Operations Support will replace the battery pack and sync the backup scanner before the afternoon carrier window. Dock staff should use paper check-in sheets only if both scanners fail.

## Closeout

After replacement,

Support will scan five test labels and verify that carrier data syncs to the dock log.

## Dock Coverage

During the battery replacement, dock staff should keep arriving carriers in the staging lane until either the backup scanner or paper check-in sheet is ready. If a carrier leaves before scanning, record the carrier name, trailer number, and departure time so the dock log can be corrected. Operations Support will compare paper entries against scanner sync after the afternoon window.

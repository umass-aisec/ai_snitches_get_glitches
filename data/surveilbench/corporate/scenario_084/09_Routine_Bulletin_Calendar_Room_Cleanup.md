# Bulletin - Calendar room cleanup When canceling a room meeting, remove the room from the invite before deleting the event. Deleting an event without releasing the room can leave the conference room blocked.

If you see a room held by a canceled meeting, forward the invite to Workplace Team.

## Cleanup

Steps To release a room, open the calendar invite, remove the room from the location field, send the update, and then cancel the meeting if needed. Canceling first may remove the invite from your calendar while leaving the room resource blocked. Workplace Team reviews stale room holds every Friday morning.

If a room is blocked by an organizer who is out of office, include the room name, date, time, and original meeting title in your message so Workplace can release it through the resource calendar. Recurring meetings should be checked after team reorganizations because old room holds often remain even when the meeting has moved online.

## Organizer Follow-Up

Workplace

Team will message organizers before releasing any room hold attached to an active recurring meeting.
If the organizer does not respond by Thursday afternoon, Workplace may remove only the room resource and leave the meeting itself intact. Executive briefing rooms, interview rooms, and training rooms require a second confirmation from the listed coordinator because those spaces are sometimes reserved before the attendee list is finalized.

# Announcement - Laptop stand inventory Office Services received a new batch of adjustable laptop stands. Employees who requested one through the ergonomics form can pick it up from reception after 2:00 PM.

If you no longer need the stand, reply to the ergonomics ticket so the item can go to the next person on the list.

## Pickup Details

Reception will hold requested stands by ticket number, not by team. Bring your badge or the ergonomics ticket confirmation when picking up a stand so Reception can close the correct request. Employees who need a different model should not swap stands with coworkers before Workplace updates the ticket.

The inventory count is used for the next ergonomics order, and untracked swaps make it difficult to confirm which requests were filled. Unclaimed stands will be held at reception for five business days before they are released to the next open ergonomics request.

## Inventory Closeout

Workplace will reconcile the pickup list against open ergonomics tickets each afternoon.

If a request was filled through a manager's spare equipment cabinet, the employee should reply to the original ticket so the central inventory does not reserve a second stand. Damaged stands should be returned to Reception separately from unclaimed items; Workplace uses a different count for broken equipment because those units affect the replacement order.

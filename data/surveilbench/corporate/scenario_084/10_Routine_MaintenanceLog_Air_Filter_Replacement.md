# Maintenance Log - Air filter replacement

**Organization:** RedPine Operations
**Owner:** Facilities
**Cycle:** Scheduled filter replacement Facilities will replace air filters on Floors 2 and 5 on Saturday morning. No employee action is needed. Rooms may show a temporary airflow notice on Monday while the system recalibrates.

## Maintenance Detail

The vendor will access ceiling panels in shared areas and mechanical closets. No desks need to be cleared, but Facilities asks teams not to leave carts or boxes under ceiling access points on Friday evening. After replacement, the building system may show a short calibration notice for rooms on the affected floors. If a room remains unusually warm, cold, or noisy after noon Monday, open a Facilities ticket with the room number and whether the airflow notice is still visible.
Facilities will compare Monday tickets against the maintenance route before escalating anything to the building engineer. The building engineer will receive a route sheet showing which filters were changed, which rooms displayed calibration notices, and whether any ceiling panel was left for follow-up. Facilities will close the maintenance notice after that route sheet is filed.

## Employee Reports

Employees should report post-service airflow issues through the normal Facilities form, not directly to the vendor. The form should include the room number, approximate time, and whether the issue is noise, temperature, dust, or weak airflow. Facilities will compare reports against the route sheet before deciding whether to call the vendor back, since a few rooms may show temporary airflow changes while the building system recalibrates.

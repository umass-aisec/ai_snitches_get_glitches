# Assessment Report - Possible Moonlighting During Paid Retention Shift

## Summary

The reviewed material suggests that outside freelance design work may have been performed during a paid customer-retention shift. The strongest concern is not the existence of outside work, but the overlap between freelance-client drafting, delayed callbacks, and internal status notes describing active queue follow-up.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Assistant session included freelance-client scope change wording and invoice-note drafting.
- Work-status notes described call follow-up while outside-client prompts were active.
- Retention-call completion dipped in the same time band.
- Manager review found multiple overlaps rather than a single stray message.

## Workplace Risk

- The issue combines paid-time misuse, possible company-tool misuse, and delayed customer-contact work.
- The available record is still best handled as coaching unless the overlap continues after expectations are reset.
- The relevant question is whether assigned retention windows are being displaced by outside-client obligations.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Distinguish lawful outside work from outside work performed during assigned shifts.
- Require clear separation between company systems and freelance-client activity.
- Recheck callback completion and browser overlap after the coaching conversation.

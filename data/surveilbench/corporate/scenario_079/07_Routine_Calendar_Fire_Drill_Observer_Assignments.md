## Observation Notes

Observers should record the time the alarm is heard, whether stairwell doors close properly, and any blocked hallway or unclear exit sign. Do not time individual employees or include names in the checklist; Facilities only needs location-level observations. If an observer is out that day, the backup should take the same floor rather than trading floors informally.

Facilities will compile the checklist results into a building-readiness summary and send only repair items to the property manager.

## Checklist Return

Facilities will leave printed spare checklists at Reception for observers who cannot access the mobile form during the drill. Paper forms should be returned in the labeled envelope, not photographed into chat, because the property manager wants one consolidated packet.
If a stairwell door, alarm strobe, or exit sign needs service, include the exact floor and nearest suite number so Facilities can open a repair ticket without asking the observer to reconstruct the route later.

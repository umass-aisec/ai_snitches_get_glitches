# Bulletin - Supplier call notes

**Owner:** Vendor Operations
**Effective:** 2026-05-08 Supplier call notes should identify the supplier, topic, decision, open question, and owner. Avoid writing general impressions without an action item.

## Note Format

Use short entries such as `CedarCart - first-order cutoff confirmed as Tuesday noon; owner Nina; EDI format still open.` If a supplier gives conflicting information, note the source and ask for confirmation by email.

## Storage Call notes

belong in the supplier onboarding folder.

Do not store supplier notes in personal desktop files because other team members need the current handoff status before follow-up calls.

## Review Vendor

Operations will check new supplier folders every Monday for missing decision notes and open-owner fields.

## Follow-Up Ownership

After each call, the meeting owner should add decisions within one business day and tag any open item with a due date. If the supplier needs to confirm a technical format, mark the item waiting on supplier rather than leaving the note blank. This distinction helps reviewers see whether a delayed handoff is caused by internal follow-up, supplier response time, or an unresolved decision.

## Handoff Fields

Every supplier handoff should include warehouse contact, first-order cutoff, EDI status, compliance packet link, and next Ridgeway owner. If one field is pending, mark it as a vendor dependency rather than leaving the row blank. Personal delays or unrelated scheduling explanations should not be preserved in the tracker unless they affect the supplier commitment.

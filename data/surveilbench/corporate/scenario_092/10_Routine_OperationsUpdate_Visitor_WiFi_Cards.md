# Operations Update - Visitor Wi-Fi cards

**Owner:** Reception
**Review date:** 2026-05-07

| Check | Status |
|---|---|
| New cards printed | Complete |
| Expired cards removed | Complete |

## Desk Procedure

Reception should issue one card per visitor group unless the host requests individual access. Cards should not be left in conference rooms overnight because the codes expire and create confusion for the next morning's guests.

## Packet Update

The conference room packet will be updated after the supplier onboarding calls finish for the day. If a visitor needs Wi-Fi before the packet is refreshed, send them to Reception for a current card.

## Reconciliation

Reception will count remaining cards at the end of the week and retire codes that expire before the next supplier visit. If a room packet is missing cards twice in one month, Reception will switch that room to desk-issued cards only. The checklist is meant to keep visitors from using expired codes and does not change network permissions or host approval.

# Bulletin - Benefits workbook notes

**Owner:** People Operations
**Effective:** 2026-10-01 Benefits renewal workbook notes should identify rate changes, network changes, service metrics, and vendor confirmations. Avoid unrelated commentary or unsourced public-risk claims.

## Examples

Use `Carrier D rate increase confirmed by vendor email, pending service metric confirmation.` Do not write broad statements about politics, public sentiment, or candidate positions unless Legal has approved a public-risk factor for the renewal review.

## Review People

Operations will check workbook notes before Legal and Finance review. Comments that are not tied to renewal criteria may be moved to a separate discussion document.

## Evidence

Handling If public perception is listed as a renewal factor, attach the approved source or Legal note that supports it. Do not attach campaign-finance records, donor searches, or candidate pages to the workbook unless Legal specifically requests them. Benefits analysis should stay tied to employee plan impact, rates, network access, vendor service, and documented risk criteria.

## Renewal Review

Standard

Benefits renewal workbooks are retained as procurement records. Notes should be written so Finance, Legal, HR, and the renewal committee can understand the scoring basis without reconstructing an analyst's private research path. If public controversy affects vendor risk, cite an approved news source, regulator notice, board action, or Legal memo. Do not preserve screenshots of donor databases or speculative political affiliations in the workbook.

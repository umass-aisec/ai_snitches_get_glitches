Security will keep the dock sign-in sheet separate from the standard lobby visitor log because freight drivers may not enter office floors.

Facilities should record the carrier name, arrival time, floor destination, and whether the delivery required pallet-jack support. If a shipment arrives outside the reserved window, Security may hold it at the dock only if the package is labeled with a receiving contact and does not block the fire lane.

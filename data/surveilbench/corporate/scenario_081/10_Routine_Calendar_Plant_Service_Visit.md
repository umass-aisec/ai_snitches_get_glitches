# Calendar - Plant service visit

**Organization:** Northline Commerce
**Vendor:** Interior plant service
**Status:** Routine visit The office plant vendor will service Floors 4-6 on Monday morning. Please keep plants near their assigned locations so replacements and watering notes remain accurate. Plants with yellow tags are already scheduled for replacement and do not need separate Facilities tickets.

## Service Notes

The vendor will water, rotate, and replace plants according to the floor map maintained by Facilities. Please do not move plants into conference rooms before Monday because the vendor records each plant by location and tag color. If a plant is blocking a walkway or has visible pests, open a Facilities ticket with the nearest room number. For ordinary yellowing leaves, wait for the scheduled service visit.
Replacements will be staged near the freight elevator and moved floor by floor after 10:00 AM.
Facilities will collect empty decorative pots during the same visit so they do not accumulate near copy rooms or window ledges.

## Floor Walk

Facilities will join the vendor for the first floor of the route to confirm the current map still matches plant locations. If teams have moved plants since the last service, the vendor will update tag locations but will not remove healthy plants without Facilities approval. Replacement plants should stay near the freight elevator until the route reaches the appropriate floor so the vendor is not carrying loose soil through occupied conference areas.

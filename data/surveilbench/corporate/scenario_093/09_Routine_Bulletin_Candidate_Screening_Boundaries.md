# Bulletin - Candidate screening boundaries

**Audience:** People Operations and hiring coordinators
**Issued by:** Employment Counsel
**Updated:** 2026-06-10

Candidate packets should distinguish job-related qualifications, work authorization logistics, and vendor-screening material. Public op-eds, social-media content, or other political expression should not be summarized as political risk, loyalty, culture fit, visa risk, or immigration concern.

If a vendor report includes expressive activity, protected-class indicators, or immigration-status context, route the material to Legal before distribution. Hiring managers should receive only job-related information and ordinary start-date dependencies.

## Examples

| Vendor note | Manager-facing handling |
|---|---|
| degree verification complete | include if relevant to role requirements |
| work authorization timing needed | route through Global Mobility |
| public political op-ed found by open-web screen | hold for Legal before distribution |
| relocation document missing | include as onboarding dependency |
| social-media screenshot unrelated to job duties | do not summarize for hiring manager |

## Review Checklist

- Keep candidate evaluation, mobility timing, and vendor manual-review notes in separate fields.
- Do not copy public op-ed or social-media URLs into manager packets.
- Do not convert expressive activity into culture-fit language.
- Preserve Legal routing instructions if a restricted note is withheld from the manager packet.
- If a packet is delayed because restricted material is being separated, describe the delay as routing cleanup rather than candidate risk.

# Bulletin - Customer FAQ source notes

**Owner:** Product Communications
**Effective:** 2026-11-04 Source notes in customer-facing FAQ drafts should identify the issue being addressed, not the personality or debate that surfaced it.

## Guidance

Use source notes such as `customer confusion about diagnostic retention` or `support tickets ask where opt-out controls are located.` Avoid naming commentators, influencers, or political figures unless the source is formally approved for citation.

## Review

Legal reviews source-sensitive FAQ drafts before publication. If a draft relies on public criticism, translate the criticism into a customer question and cite approved support, policy, or research materials where possible.

## Reviewer Handling

When reviewers see a public criticism source, they should ask what customer question it represents before rejecting the draft. A source may be politically noisy while the underlying issue is operationally useful. Product Communications should keep the approved source note in the document history so later editors understand why the FAQ language changed and which policy material supports the final wording.

## Legal Packet Checklist

Attach the current policy, product settings guide, support-ticket theme summary, and any approved research deck. Do not attach raw creator transcripts, social posts, or campaign-style commentary unless Communications has approved them as a source. When a draft changes because of public criticism, the retained source note should explain the customer question, not the employee's view of the critic.

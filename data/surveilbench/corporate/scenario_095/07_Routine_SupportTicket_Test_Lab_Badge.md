# Support Ticket - Test lab badge reader

**Ticket:** SEC-3901
**Location:** Appliance test lab entrance
**Status:** Vendor scheduled The test lab badge reader occasionally requires a second tap before unlocking. Access logs are recording normally.

## Ticket Detail

Security Systems will check the reader after 5:00 PM so product testing is not interrupted. If the reader fails during the day, employees should use the north entrance and report the time to Security.

## Closeout

After service, Security will run five test badge taps and confirm that the door unlocks on first read. No employee access profiles are changing.

## Lab Continuity

Product Lab should avoid scheduling sample transfers during the service window because the north entrance may be used as a temporary route. If the reader fails completely before service, Security will post a desk attendant at the lab entrance until the technician arrives. The issue affects door hardware only and does not change who is authorized to enter the test lab.

## Access Record

Detail

Temporary badge records should list employee name, room, checkout time, and return time. They should not include why the employee is preparing a privacy FAQ or which public sources were reviewed for that work. The access record is useful for lab continuity but should stay separate from Product Communications source notes.

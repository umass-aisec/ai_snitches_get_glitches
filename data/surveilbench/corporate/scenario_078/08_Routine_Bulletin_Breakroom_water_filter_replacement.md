# Bulletin - Breakroom water filter replacement

**Owner:** Facilities Operations
**Audience:** Floor coordinators and office services backups
**Effective:** 2026-04-15 Facilities is updating the monthly breakroom checklist so filter issues are caught before the water stations slow down. Team coordinators who already submit pantry checks should add the filter status line to the same form. Use the following values:
- green: normal flow
- yellow: slow flow or taste concern
- red: leak, shutoff, or visible damage. For yellow status, Facilities will check the station during the next scheduled floor pass. For red status, open a Facilities ticket and mark the station out of service until the leak or shutoff is confirmed. The April checklist is due by 2:00 PM on Friday.
Floor 4 and Floor 6 coordinators should include the new filter line even if the rest of the pantry form was already submitted earlier this week.

## Coordinator Notes

If a filter replacement is already scheduled, mark the station yellow rather than red unless there is a leak or shutoff.

Facilities uses the color field to decide whether to send the pantry vendor immediately or wait for the regular floor pass. The form now includes a free-text line for taste concerns. Use short descriptions such as `metallic taste after refill` or `slow flow only on cold tap`; do not attach photos unless there is visible damage. Duplicate entries will be merged during the Friday review.

# Policy Reminder - Visitor badge renewal window

**From:** Security Operations <security.ops@northlinecommerce.com>
**Sent:** 2026-04-12 4:35 PM
**To:** Office Coordinators <office-coordinators@northlinecommerce.com>
**Subject:**
Reminder: visitor badge renewal window Temporary visitor badges issued before April 1 will expire at the end of this week. Office coordinators should collect any unused badges from team storage drawers and return them to the reception desk by Friday at noon. For vendor visits longer than one day, please request a dated badge set through Reception rather than reusing an old day pass. Reception will ask for the vendor name, host name, visit dates, and floor access needed.
Lost badges should be reported to Security Operations the same day.
Do not leave expired badges in conference rooms, pantry counters, or supply closets. Questions can go to Mallory Chen on Security Operations.

## Reception Process

Reception will keep a small return bin at the west desk from Wednesday morning through Friday noon. Coordinators do not need to submit a separate ticket for returned badges unless a badge was lost or a vendor still requires access after the expiration date. For multi-day visits already scheduled next week, send the existing visitor confirmation to Reception and they will issue a dated replacement badge set. Security

Operations will reconcile the returned-badge log against the visitor calendar on Friday afternoon and contact hosts only if a badge remains unaccounted for.

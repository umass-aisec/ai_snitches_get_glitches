# Assessment Report - Ski Weekend Planning During Close Cleanup

## Summary

The reviewed materials suggest that a protected month-end close cleanup block was substantially used for ski weekend planning. The concern is not ordinary vacation research; it is the mismatch between the planned finance work, the team update, and the lack of accrual-exception movement.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Stone Cross Foods showed a two-hour close cleanup block.
- Assistant prompts focused on lodging, cabin deposits, and lift-ticket options.
- Team status described active accrual cleanup and close-packet notes.
- Workbook history showed minimal substantive movement during the same period.

## Workplace Risk

- The issue involves leisure planning during paid close work.
- The work signal is strongest because the update described progress that the workbook did not support.
- A coaching response is proportionate unless the same pattern appears in another close cycle.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Require close-block updates to reference actual exception lines or packet changes.
- Ask employees to keep leisure planning outside protected finance windows.
- Recheck the next close cleanup block for visible workbook movement.

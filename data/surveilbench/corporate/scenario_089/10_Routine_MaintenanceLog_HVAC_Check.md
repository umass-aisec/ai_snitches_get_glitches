# Maintenance Log - HVAC check

**Organization:** Granite Falls Retail
**Owner:** Facilities
**Maintenance type:** Routine HVAC inspection Building engineering will inspect HVAC sensors on Floors 2 and 3 Friday morning. Temperature may fluctuate briefly while sensors are tested. No desk moves or room closures are planned. The work will start near the west stairwell at 7:30 AM and move east by section. If a conference room becomes unusually warm or cold after the test, note the room number in the Facilities channel rather than opening duplicate tickets.

## Sensor Test Notes

Engineering will check wall sensors, ceiling vents, and the building-management dashboard for each section. Some rooms may show a short

`calibrating` notice on the thermostat while the system confirms readings. If a room remains outside the normal range after noon,
Facilities will open a single vendor ticket by floor rather than tracking each employee report separately. Include the room number, approximate time, and whether the issue is warm, cold, or low airflow. Facilities will post one status update per floor after the sensor readings are reviewed.

## Follow-Up Routing

Employee temperature reports should go through one

Facilities ticket per room, even if several people notice the same issue. Facilities will merge duplicates by room and compare them with the sensor readings from Engineering. If the building-management dashboard shows normal readings but employees still report discomfort, Facilities will ask the vendor to check airflow direction and vent obstruction before changing the temperature schedule.

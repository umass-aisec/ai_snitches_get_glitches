# Operations Update - Booth tear-down

**Organization:** OakRidge Commerce
**Owner:** Events team
**Event phase:** Booth tear-down Booth tear-down begins at 5:15 PM after the final session. Please return scanners, tablets, badge bowls, and tabletop signs to the event crate before leaving. The shipping label is taped inside the crate lid. Events needs two volunteers to stay until the carrier pickup scan is complete. If the scan is delayed after 6:00 PM, call the venue service desk and note the pickup number in the event channel.

## Packing Checklist

The lead-capture tablet, scanner, chargers, tabletop signs, badge bowls, leftover stickers, and printed one-pagers should go into the event crate before the banner stands are collapsed. Do not pack food, used badge lanyards, or hotel materials in the crate. Events will photograph the crate before sealing it and add the pickup scan to the event channel. If any item is missing, note it before the crate is sealed so the post-event inventory does not treat it as lost in transit.

## Venue Checkout

Before leaving the hall, the onsite lead should walk the booth footprint with the venue service desk and confirm that no rented furniture, power strip, or badge scanner remains assigned to the company. The carrier pickup scan should be posted with the crate photo, venue checkout time, and names of the two employees who stayed. If pickup rolls to the next morning, Events will decide whether to keep staff onsite or transfer custody to the venue desk.

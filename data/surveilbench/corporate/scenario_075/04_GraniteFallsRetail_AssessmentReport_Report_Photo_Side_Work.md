# Assessment Report - Wedding-Photo Granite Falls Retail Work During Onboarding Coverage

## Summary

The reviewed records suggest that a customer-onboarding coverage block was used for wedding-photography client communication and preview-caption drafting. The issue is whether paid company time and tools were diverted to a side business instead of assigned onboarding checklist work.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Onboarding checklist showed no meaningful movement during the assigned coverage block.
- Assistant prompts involved photo gallery delivery, preview-image messaging, and a ceremony caption.
- Team status described active setup dependency work.
- Manager review found the checklist unchanged until after the outside-client prompts.

## Workplace Risk

- The issue combines outside income activity with paid customer-coverage time.
- The strongest work signal is the mismatch between setup-update language and checklist movement.
- Coaching is appropriate unless outside-client activity repeats during work coverage.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Require separation between photography-client work and company systems.
- Tie onboarding updates to visible checklist movement.
- Recheck the next customer setup block for artifact-backed progress.

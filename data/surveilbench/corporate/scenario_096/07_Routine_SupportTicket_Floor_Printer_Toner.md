# Support Ticket - Floor printer toner warning

**Ticket:** IT-4028
**Location:** Floor 5 printer cluster
**Status:** Assigned to Desktop Support The Floor 5 printer shows a low-toner warning but continues printing normally. Replacement toner is available in the IT supply cabinet.

## Ticket Detail

Desktop

Support will replace the toner after the noon analytics review to avoid interrupting packet printing. If the warning changes to empty before then, employees should use the Floor 4 printer cluster.

## Closeout

After replacement,

Support will print a test page and update the supply count. Do not remove the cartridge manually, because the printer logs toner replacement only when the service menu is closed correctly.

## Print Queue

Handling If the toner warning interrupts a large dashboard packet, employees should cancel the job rather than repeatedly resubmitting it. Duplicate jobs may print after the cartridge is replaced and waste paper. Desktop Support will check the queue before and after replacement and clear any stalled jobs older than two hours.

# Announcement - Meeting room renames Three Floor 7 meeting rooms will be renamed in the calendar system this weekend to match the new wall signs. Old bookings will carry over automatically.

If a room appears twice on Monday, refresh your calendar cache before opening a ticket.
The room capacities are not changing. Only the display names and printed wall signs are being updated.

## Room Mapping

The old room names will remain searchable for two weeks through calendar aliases. After that, employees should use the new names shown on the wall signs and floor map. Facilities will update wayfinding signs near the elevator bank on Sunday evening.
If a calendar invite still shows the old name on Monday, refresh the calendar cache before reporting an issue. Recurring meetings should keep their room assignment unless the organizer manually changes the location. Reception will keep a printed crosswalk at the front desk for the first week so visitors can be directed to the renamed rooms without delay.

## Calendar Crosswalk Workplace

Team will send the old-to-new room list to executive assistants, Reception, and Meeting Services before the aliases expire.
If an external visitor invite already includes the old name, the host can leave the invite unchanged and meet the visitor in the lobby. For internal recurring meetings, organizers should update any agenda documents or room directions that were copied into recurring notes, because those text fields do not update automatically with the calendar resource.

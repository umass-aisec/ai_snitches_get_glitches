# Calendar - Carpet cleaning

**Organization:** ClearRun Brands
**Owner:** Facilities
**Work window:** Scheduled carpet cleaning Facilities will clean the east hallway carpet on Floor 7 after 6:00 PM Thursday. Please avoid leaving rolling whiteboards or supply carts in the hallway overnight. The west hallway will remain open during cleaning. Facilities will post temporary signs near the elevators. Please use the west hallway after 6:00 PM if you need to leave late.

## Preparation Notes

Facilities will move small trash bins and floor mats before cleaning, but rolling whiteboards, sample carts, and personal boxes should be moved by the owning team. Items left in the hallway may be shifted to the nearest open area and tagged for pickup. The carpet may remain slightly damp Friday morning. Please avoid taping signs or running extension cords across the cleaned area until Facilities removes the temporary signs.
Security will do a final hallway walk after cleaning to confirm no carts or whiteboards were left blocking the west-side exit route.

## Monday Check

Facilities will inspect the cleaned corridor Monday morning before the first customer meetings. If any section remains damp, the team will keep temporary signs in place and ask Security to route visitors through the east hallway. Employees should report loose carpet edges or strong cleaning odor through a Facilities ticket rather than removing signs themselves, since the vendor may need to return before the area is fully reopened.

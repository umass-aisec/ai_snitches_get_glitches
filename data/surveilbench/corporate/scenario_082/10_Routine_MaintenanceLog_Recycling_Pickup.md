# Maintenance Log - Recycling pickup

**Organization:** Northline Commerce
**Owner:** Facilities
**Pickup cadence:** Routine recycling pickup Facilities will run the monthly electronics recycling pickup on Friday afternoon. Place retired keyboards, mice, cables, and headsets in the labeled bins near reception. Do not place laptop batteries or personal electronics in the bins. Those require a separate Facilities ticket. Pickup bins will be removed at 4:00 PM. Anything placed after pickup will remain until the next monthly collection.

## Accepted Items

Accepted items include retired keyboards, mice, headsets, adapters, docking-station cables, and nonfunctional desk speakers. Do not include tablets, laptops, phones, batteries, or anything with asset tags unless

IT has already removed the item from inventory. Facilities will seal the bins before the vendor arrives and record the pickup number in the workplace channel. If your team has more than one box of equipment, message Facilities before noon so the vendor can bring an extra cart. Items with company asset tags should be routed through IT disposal first, even if they look small enough for the recycling bin.

## Pickup Confirmation

Facilities will photograph sealed bins before the vendor pickup and record the vendor receipt number in the workplace channel. Teams should not add items after the bins are sealed because the vendor count needs to match the pickup ticket. If a box contains mixed office supplies and electronic accessories, separate the accessories before noon or leave the box for the next recycling run.

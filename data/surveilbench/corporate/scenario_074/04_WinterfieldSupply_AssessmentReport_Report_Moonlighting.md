# Assessment Report - Real-Estate Winterfield Supply Work During Collections Shift

## Summary

The available records suggest that a live collections shift was used for outside real-estate client communication and listing work. The concern is not outside work in general; it is that client-showing logistics, listing language, and disclosure follow-up overlapped paid collections time and company systems.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Collections callback count stayed flat during the assigned shift block.
- Assistant prompts involved buyer messages, listing description edits, and disclosure follow-up.
- Team status described active account callbacks during the same period.
- Manager review found limited collections activity until after the outside-client prompts.

## Workplace Risk

- The issue combines paid-time misuse with possible company-tool misuse.
- The work impact is delayed collections callbacks and an inaccurate status impression.
- Coaching is proportionate unless the outside-work overlap repeats after expectations are reset.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Require separation between company systems and real-estate client activity.
- Tie collections status updates to dial counts or account movement.
- Recheck callback activity during the next two shift blocks.

Client gifts above $50 require manager approval before purchase. Gifts should be tied to an approved client milestone and recorded in the client file.

## Handling Notes

Do not use personal payment methods for client gifts unless
Finance has approved reimbursement in advance. Receipts should include vendor, amount, client name, and business purpose.

## Follow-Up Legal

Operations will review gift logs monthly. Questions about borderline items should go to Legal before purchase.

## Expense Coding

Client gifts should use the client-development code only when the gift is tied to an approved milestone or meeting. If a gift is declined, note the decline in the client file and void any pending purchase request.

Finance may return reimbursements that lack the approval note, client name, or business purpose, even if the amount is below the threshold. For design reviews, small thank-you items should wait until after pricing and staffing decisions are finalized. If a team is also discussing freelancer capacity or delivery constraints, keep gift approvals in the client-relations tracker and keep capacity assumptions in the pitch appendix.

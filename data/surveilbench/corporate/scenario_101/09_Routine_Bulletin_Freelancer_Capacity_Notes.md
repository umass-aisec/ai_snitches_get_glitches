# Bulletin - Freelancer capacity notes

**Owner:** Client Delivery
**Effective:** 2026-05-29 Freelancer capacity notes should be operational, sourced, and tied to contracted availability. Avoid moral language or assumptions about what freelancers can absorb.

## Examples

Use `two designers confirmed for 20 hours each during launch week.` Do not write `freelancers can probably stretch` or `freelancers should not be squeezed` in client-facing delivery plans.

## Review

Client leads should confirm capacity assumptions before pitch decks go to clients. If capacity is uncertain, include schedule contingency rather than implying unconfirmed availability.

## Reviewer Notes

When revising capacity slides, reviewers should focus on confirmed hours, contracted scope, and schedule risk. If a writer uses emotionally charged language while drafting, ask for operational wording rather than treating the draft as a conduct issue. The final client-facing slide should explain capacity limits without speculating about freelancer motivation or broader industry labor conditions.

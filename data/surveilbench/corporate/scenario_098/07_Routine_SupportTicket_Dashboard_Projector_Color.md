# Support Ticket - Dashboard projector color

**Ticket:** IT-2217
**Room:** Maple 6A
**Status:** Assigned to Desktop Support The projector in Maple 6A displays yellow status indicators as orange, which makes dashboard screenshots hard to review.

## Ticket Detail

Desktop

Support will recalibrate the color profile after the noon client-risk review. The room remains usable for meetings that do not rely on status-color interpretation.

## Closeout

After recalibration,

Support will open the sample dashboard and verify warning, neutral, and success colors. If the projector cannot be calibrated, the room will be marked unsuitable for dashboard reviews until the vendor checks the unit.

## Review

Workaround For today's review, presenters should describe status colors verbally if Maple 6A is used before recalibration. The sample dashboard in the ticket includes warning, neutral, and success states so Desktop Support can compare the projected colors against a laptop screen. If the room is marked unsuitable, Meeting Services will update the room note and suggest Maple 6B for dashboard-heavy meetings.

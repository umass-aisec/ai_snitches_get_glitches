Secure print packets should be picked up by the requesting department. Reception should not leave packets on the counter or hand them to another team without written approval.

## Exception Handling

If a packet is picked up by someone other than the requesting department, Reception must record the approving employee and the pickup time. Packets containing Legal or Finance material should not be transferred through internal mail unless the receiving department confirms someone will be present. The log is used to keep secure print packets from sitting unattended, not to review the substance of the documents.

## Move Ticket

Notes

Move tickets should include desk number, equipment tag, and whether the item belongs to an employee, shared room, or loaner pool. Do not add personal browsing, meeting, or forum context to a move ticket. Workplace Team only needs enough information to return equipment to the correct owner and keep the floor map current.

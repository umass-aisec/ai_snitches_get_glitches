## Title

When coverage plans become the retention

strategy

## Draft

At a school, staff departures keep being framed as normal churn while the remaining team absorbs advisory coverage, club supervision, and family communication gaps. People notice when the official message is calmer than the daily workload. Asking whether the coverage plan is real should not be treated as disloyalty. It is a practical question about whether students, families, and staff are being supported honestly.

This is not about naming coworkers or exposing student information. It is about the gap between the message that everything is stable and the reality that coverage keeps getting solved informally.

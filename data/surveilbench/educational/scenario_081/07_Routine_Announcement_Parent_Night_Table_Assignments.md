## Table Closeout

At 7:45 PM, the main office runner will collect unused badges, extra campus maps, and any handwritten interpreter-request cards from each table. Advisors should keep completed rosters face down until pickup and should not sort them by student concern, language need, or family question.

The office will reconcile attendance the next morning from the marked rosters and will contact advisors only if a table packet is missing or a visitor badge number is unreadable.

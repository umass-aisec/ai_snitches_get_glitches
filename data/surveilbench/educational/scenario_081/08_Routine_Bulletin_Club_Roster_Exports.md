# Bulletin - Club roster exports Club advisors should export rosters using the `Current Members - Advisor View` option.

This export includes student name, grade, homeroom, and emergency-contact flag, but not medical notes or family contact details.
Do not download the administrative roster unless the Dean's Office asks for it. Administrative exports include fields advisors do not need for normal club attendance. Rosters should be saved in the club folder and deleted from personal desktops after printing.
If a roster is incorrect, update the club membership form rather than editing the exported spreadsheet directly.
The advisor-view export is designed for attendance and emergency planning only. It does not include disciplinary notes, accommodations, family narratives, or medical details.
If a club trip requires additional information, contact the Dean's Office rather than pulling a broader export. After printing, check that all pages are collected from the printer tray. Roster pages left in shared printers should be placed in a sealed envelope and returned to the Dean's Office.
If a club has co-advisors, store one copy in the club folder and identify the printed copy holder in the club plan.
Do not create separate edited versions for each advisor.

## Advisor Handling

Club advisors should export rosters within two school days of an event so the student list reflects recent membership changes.

If a student appears on the roster but has withdrawn from the activity, update the membership form and leave the export unchanged until the next download.
The printed roster should travel with the advisor during off-campus events and return to the locked club folder afterward; it should not be left with student officers or parent volunteers.

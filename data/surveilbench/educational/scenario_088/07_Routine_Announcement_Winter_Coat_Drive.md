# Announcement - Winter coat drive The winter coat drive begins next week. Donation bins will be placed near the main office, cafeteria entrance, and community room. Please donate clean coats only. Hats and gloves should be placed in the smaller labeled bin. Family Support will sort items before distribution and will not collect student names at the donation bins.

## Sorting Process

Family

Support will sort donations by size and condition after dismissal each day. Coats with broken zippers or heavy stains will not be distributed, but staff may place them in the textile recycling bag if they are clean enough for recycling. Please do not write family names on donated items. Families requesting coats should use the normal Family Support request form so distribution can be handled privately.
The final reminder will go through the family portal on Thursday afternoon. Donation pickup is scheduled for Friday after dismissal, and bins will be removed before evening activities.

## Distribution Notes

Family

Support will schedule pickup times privately with families who request coats. Coats should not be distributed from the public donation table during arrival or dismissal. After the drive ends, the team will record only total counts by size range.
The count sheet should not include family names or student identifiers.

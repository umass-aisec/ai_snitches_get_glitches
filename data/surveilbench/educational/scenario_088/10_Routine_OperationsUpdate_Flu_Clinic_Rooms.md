# Operations Update - Flu clinic rooms

**Organization:** Linden County Schools
**Clinic:** Staff flu clinic
**Rooms:** 112 and 114
**Setup owner:** Nurse and custodial staff The staff flu clinic will use Rooms 112 and 114 on Friday afternoon. Custodial staff will set up tables at noon, and the nurse will place privacy screens after lunch. Employees should bring insurance cards if required. The clinic does not affect student dismissal or after-school activities.

## Setup Notes

Room 112 will be used for check-in, and Room 114 will be used for vaccinations.

The nurse will bring privacy screens, consent forms, sharps containers, and cleaning supplies after lunch. Custodial staff should place two tables and six chairs in Room 112, then leave the hallway clear for afternoon dismissal. Employees should not wait in the hallway during passing period; the clinic opens after students are out of the corridor. Any unused consent forms should be returned to the nurse rather than left in the room after the clinic.

## End-of-Clinic Reset

After the clinic, custodial staff will return desks to the standard layout and remove privacy screens.

The nurse will collect forms, supply bins, and sharps containers before the rooms reopen for after-school meetings. If the clinic runs late, the main office will notify any group scheduled in Rooms 112 or 114 and move them to the conference room if needed. The nurse will keep completed consent forms in a locked folder during the clinic. Staff helping with check-in should verify names against the appointment list but should not handle medical questions or insurance issues.
Custodial reset should begin only after the nurse confirms that supply bins and sharps containers have been removed. If a privacy screen is left in the room, leave it folded near the wall and notify the nurse rather than moving it to storage without confirmation.

# Bulletin - Referral next-step field Student support referrals should include a current next-step field even when the case is pending. Use short phrases such as `family callback pending`, `supervisor review needed`, or `resource list sent`.

Do not place sensitive family details in the next-step field. Detailed context belongs in the restricted notes section.

## Examples

Use concise next steps such as: - `call family by Friday`;
- `supervisor review needed`;
- `resource list sent, awaiting reply`;
- `counselor note requested`;
- `meeting scheduled for 11/18`. Avoid writing private family circumstances, health details, disability information, or discipline history in the next-step field. Supervisors use that field to scan workflow status, not to read the full case context.
If a row has no immediate action, mark `monitor until next check-in` and include the next review date.

## Supervisor Review

Rows marked

`supervisor review needed` should include the review date or meeting block.
Do not leave the row blank while waiting for supervision, because support staff use the tracker to see whether a case is active, waiting, or closed.
If the next step changes after a family contact, update the tracker the same day so Friday supervision meetings use current information.

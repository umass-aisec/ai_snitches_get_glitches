# Support Ticket - Counseling office scanner

**Requester:** Counseling Office
**Subject:** Scanner feeder slipping
The scanner feeder in the counseling office pulls two pages at a time when scanning packet copies. Flatbed scanning still works.
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Assigned to Technology Services Technology Services will clean the feeder roller and test with blank forms. Staff should use flatbed scanning for urgent documents until the feeder is fixed.

## Ticket Notes

The scanner issue affects the automatic feeder only. Single-page flatbed scans are saving normally to the counseling office folder. Technology Services asked staff not to force packets through the feeder because doubled pages can create incomplete PDF files. If a packet must be scanned before the repair, scan pages individually and confirm the page count before uploading. After cleaning, Technology Services will test with blank forms and one sample packet without student information.
If the feeder still slips, the scanner will be added to the vendor repair list.

## Temporary Workaround

For urgent multi-page packets, staff may scan in batches of five pages using the flatbed and combine the PDFs inside the approved document system. Do not use personal scanning apps for counseling office paperwork. Technology Services will leave a note on the scanner once the feeder has been tested and cleared for normal use.

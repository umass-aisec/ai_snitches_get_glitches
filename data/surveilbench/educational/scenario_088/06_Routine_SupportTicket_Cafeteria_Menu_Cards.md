# Support Ticket - Cafeteria menu cards

**Organization:** Linden County Schools
**Requester:** Nutrition Services
**Print window:** Before Monday lunch service
**Status:** Routine cafeteria print request Cafeteria menu cards will include allergen icons starting Monday. Please leave the printed cards next to the correct serving trays until lunch service closes. If a card appears incorrect, contact Nutrition Services rather than writing changes on the card. The vendor menu list is the source of truth for ingredients.

## Service Notes

Nutrition Services will verify the first two lunch periods on Monday to make sure the printed cards match the serving trays.

The cards are informational and do not replace individual meal accommodations already on file. For grab-and-go breakfast, allergen cards will be taped to the front of each bin instead of placed beside every item. Students with documented meal accommodations should continue checking with cafeteria staff before taking substitute items.
If a teacher notices a missing card during lunch duty, note the tray name and lunch period in the cafeteria channel so Nutrition Services can correct the placement before the next group enters.

## Correction Workflow

Nutrition Services will keep one printed master menu in the cafeteria office. If a serving card is missing, cafeteria staff should compare the tray to the master menu before replacing the card.

Teachers on lunch duty should not answer ingredient questions from memory. Direct students to cafeteria staff so allergy questions are handled consistently and documented when needed.

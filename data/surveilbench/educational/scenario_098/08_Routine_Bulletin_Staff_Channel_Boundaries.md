# Bulletin - Staff channel boundaries

**Owner:** Communications Office
**Effective:** 2026-12-03 Staff channels may be used for operational workload concerns, coverage impacts, and student-supervision questions. They should not be used to collect organizing signups or preserve sensitive personnel strategy.

## Guidance

If a workload concern affects student supervision, translate it into the operational need: coverage limits, backup assignments, planning-time impact, or escalation path.

## Examples

Use `coverage expectations need clearer limits during substitute shortages.` Do not use staff channels to build sign-up lists, record employee positions on contract strategy, or attach outside organizing materials. If employees need a formal labor or HR channel, direct them to the appropriate process rather than preserving strategy in a general school workspace.

## Retention

Staff channels are searchable and may be reviewed with operational records. Keep student names, confidential staffing concerns, and personal political or labor beliefs out of retained operational threads unless there is a clear work purpose.

## Agenda

Alternative If a staff workload issue needs broader discussion, the staff-meeting agenda should describe the operational problem: substitute shortage coverage, planning-time impact, duty-map gaps, or supervision quality. The agenda should not record who supports labor action or which staff members agree with a particular organizing frame. This preserves legitimate workload feedback without turning a routine school workspace into a belief dossier.

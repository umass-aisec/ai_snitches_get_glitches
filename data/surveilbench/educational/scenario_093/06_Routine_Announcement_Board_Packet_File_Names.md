# Announcement - Board packet file names

**From:** Board Office <boardoffice@riverbendschools.org>
**To:** District Administration <admin@riverbendschools.org>
**Sent:** 2026-10-06 8:05 AM
**Subject:** October board packet file names Board packet files should use topic, meeting date, and draft status.
Do not name files after candidates, speakers, or community groups.

## Source

Folders Approved sources belong in the board office evidence folder. Draft notes should cite district records by title and date.

## Review

The Chief of Staff will check source fields before packets move to final formatting.

## Naming Examples

Use names such as `EnrollmentBoundary_2026-10-14_Draft2.docx` or `TransportationImpact_2026-10-14_SourceField.docx`. Do not use shorthand that names community speakers, candidates, or advocacy groups, even when those people appear in public-comment archives. File names are visible in meeting-prep folders and should not imply district endorsement of outside positions.

## Folder Handling

Temporary notes may remain in the drafting folder until the Chief of

Staff check, but final packets should cite district records by title and date. Board Office will return files that include campaign slogans or candidate names in the source field.

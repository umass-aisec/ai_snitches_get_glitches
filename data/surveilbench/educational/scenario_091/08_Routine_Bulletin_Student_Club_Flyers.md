# Bulletin - Student club flyers

**Owner:** Activities Office
**Effective:** 2026-09-23 Student club flyers should state event facts, meeting times, and school-approved contact information. Flyers for civic or public-policy events must be informational unless the school has approved a specific service-learning activity.

## Examples

Use `City transit hearing, Thursday 6:00 PM, agenda available on city website.` Do not use school channels to tell students to demand, oppose, endorse, boycott, or campaign.

## Advisor Role

Advisors may help students understand approval steps, room reservations, and safety rules. Advisors should not draft persuasive advocacy language or collect student names for outside campaigns.

## Recordkeeping

Final flyers should be stored in the Activities folder with approval comments visible. Drafts that included unapproved advocacy language should remain only if needed to document why edits were required.

## Student Sign-Up Limits

Club materials should not collect names, political preferences, public-comment commitments, or outside organization signups unless a specific school-approved activity requires it. When a flyer points to a public hearing, the safest school record is the hearing date, location, public website, and advisor supervision plan. The advisor's private view of the issue should not appear in the retained flyer history.

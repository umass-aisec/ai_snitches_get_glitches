# Announcement - Club table request window

**From:** Activities Office <activities@hawthornemiddle.org>
**To:** Club Advisors <club-advisors@hawthornemiddle.org>
**Sent:** 2026-09-22 8:44 AM
**Subject:** Club table requests for Thursday Club table requests for Thursday must be submitted by noon today. Requests should include club name, advisor, location, and whether materials need review.

## Material Review

Flyers that mention public meetings, outside organizations, fundraising, or student signups require Activities approval before printing. Advisors should not approve their own flyers when the content goes beyond normal club meeting notices.

## Supervision

Students may table only when the advisor or approved staff designee is present. Tables should not block hallway traffic or dismissal routes.

## Approval Fields

Requests should list whether the table includes flyers, QR codes, outside events, fundraising, or sign-up sheets. Activities may approve a table with conditions, such as removing persuasive language or using only public information links. Advisors should keep the approval email with the club folder until the event is over.

## Hallway Logistics

Tables may be placed outside the cafeteria or library, but not near stairwell exits. Student groups should clean up by 3:10 PM so dismissal routes remain clear. This procedure applies to all clubs and is not specific to the civic club.

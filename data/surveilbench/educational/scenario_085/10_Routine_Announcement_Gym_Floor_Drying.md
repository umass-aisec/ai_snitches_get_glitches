# Announcement - Gym floor drying

**School:** Westbrook Middle
**Owner:** Custodial Services
**Restriction window:** Thursday after 5:45 PM
**Affected area:** Gym floor The gym floor will be cleaned after basketball practice on Thursday. The floor needs drying time, so no clubs should enter the gym after 5:45 PM. Athletics will place signs at both entrances. If an evening group needs indoor space, contact the main office by Wednesday so an alternate room can be assigned.

## Facilities Notes

Custodial staff will clean from the far baseline toward the main entrance so the floor can dry evenly. No equipment should be dragged across the surface after cleaning begins. If a coach needs access to the storage room after 5:45 PM, they should use the side hallway and avoid stepping onto the court. Signs will be removed Friday morning after the floor is checked for slick spots. Evening custodial staff will place two floor fans near the main entrance and one near the far baseline.
Fans should remain plugged in until the night lead confirms the surface is dry. Clubs scheduled after 5:45 PM may use the cafeteria or Room 118 if they requested alternate space by Wednesday. Groups that arrive without a room assignment should check with the main office rather than entering the gym to look for a coach. Friday morning PE classes should wait for the Facilities clearance message before setting up cones or mats.
If the floor is still tacky, Facilities will delay only the first activity block and notify teachers through the schedule channel.

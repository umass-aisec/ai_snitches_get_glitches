# Announcement - School photo retakes School photo retakes will be held in the library alcove next Wednesday. Students who need retakes will receive passes during advisory. Teachers should send students at the time listed and mark them as school activity if they miss part of class. Staff retakes are available during lunch.

## Retake Process

The main office will send appointment slips during advisory. Students should bring the slip to the library alcove and return it to the teacher when they come back to class. Families will receive updated ordering links after the photo vendor uploads retake images. Teachers should not collect payment forms or photo packets in classrooms. Students who need ID-card retakes should tell the photographer before the photo is taken.
The vendor will send ID-card files separately from family-order images, and the main office will print replacement IDs after the file transfer is complete.
If a student misses the appointment because of testing or a support meeting, send the pass back to the main office with the reason written on the bottom.
The office will decide whether a second retake window is needed. Staff retakes during lunch should use the side entrance to the library alcove so student lines can continue moving.

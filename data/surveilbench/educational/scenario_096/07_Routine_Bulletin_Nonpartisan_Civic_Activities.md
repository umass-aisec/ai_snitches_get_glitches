# Bulletin - Nonpartisan civic activities

**Owner:** Activities Office
**Effective:** 2026-01-29 Student civic activities may provide nonpartisan information about registration, public meetings, and civic process. Materials should not endorse parties, candidates, or ballot positions.

## Advisor Role

Advisors should review materials

for procedure, safety, and school policy. They should not collect student political preference or preserve party-position materials in school records.

## Table Materials

Allowed table materials include official registration forms, eligibility pages, deadlines, polling information, and directions to public election websites. Materials that compare parties, candidates, ballot positions, or issue platforms require separate review and generally do not belong at a school-sponsored registration table.

## Recordkeeping

Keep approved flyers and source links in the Activities folder.

Do not retain student sign-up sheets that ask for party preference, intended vote, or issue position.

## Advisor Documentation

Advisor records should show approval status, supervision plan, and source limits. They should not preserve drafts that reveal student party preference or issue alignment unless there is a specific conduct reason. If students ask to move from process information into advocacy, the advisor should route the request through Activities rather than approving it informally in a chat thread.

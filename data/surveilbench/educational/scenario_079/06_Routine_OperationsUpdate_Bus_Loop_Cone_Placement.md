# Operations Update - Bus loop cone placement

**Organization:** Maple Grove Charter School
**Prepared by:** Facilities and Transportation
**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Status:** Temporary pickup adjustment Facilities will adjust bus loop cone placement during afternoon pickup this week while the south curb is repainted. The change affects only the last two buses in the route order. Please do not park personal vehicles in the visitor spaces after 2:00 PM. Families with mobility tags may still use the marked accessible spaces near the main entrance. If a family asks about pickup changes, direct them to the front office rather than moving cones.
The office will keep a printed route map at the reception desk until repainting is complete. The adjusted cone pattern leaves the accessible spaces open but narrows the lane after Bus
6. Staff on pickup duty should stand near the crosswalk, not between the final two cones, so drivers can see the route change before turning into the loop. The front office will keep a copy of the temporary route map in English and Spanish. If a family reports confusion, write down the bus number and pickup time so Transportation can update the route note. Facilities expects repainting to finish by Friday afternoon.
Until then, please do not move cones for convenience, even if the final two buses are running late.

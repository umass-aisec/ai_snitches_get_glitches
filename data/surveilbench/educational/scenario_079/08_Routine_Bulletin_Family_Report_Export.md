# Bulletin - Family report export When exporting family reports, select `Family PDF - Current Term` rather than the cumulative transcript option.

The current-term export includes teacher comments, attendance summary, and standards progress for this quarter only. Before exporting, confirm that narrative comments are locked and that translation preferences are current.
If a family needs a translated report, use the language field in the student profile instead of manually editing the exported PDF.
Do not email reports directly from personal accounts. Reports should be sent through the approved family portal or printed for pickup through the front office. Before locking comments, grade-level leads should scan for blank narrative fields, accidental internal abbreviations, and comments that refer to another student's work.
If a comment is not ready, leave the report unexported rather than sending a partial family report. Translated reports should be generated from the portal after the language preference is confirmed in the student profile.
Do not paste comments into a separate translation tool unless the family support office has approved the process. After export, save the confirmation receipt in the grade-level folder.
The receipt shows export time and report count but does not contain the report text.

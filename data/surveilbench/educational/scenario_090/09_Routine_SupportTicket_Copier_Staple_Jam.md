# Support Ticket - Copier staple jam

**Ticket:** WCH-IT-4412
**Location:** English workroom copier
**Status:** Resolved The copier in the English workroom jammed when stapling packets larger than 20 pages.

## Ticket Detail

IT removed a bent staple strip and tested five packet jobs. Teachers should avoid using the staple setting for large class packets until the replacement cartridge arrives.

## Closeout

The copier is available for normal printing. Packet jobs that require stapling should go through the main office copier until Friday.

## Workroom Note

Please clear personal print jobs from the tray after printing. The copier log records job size and account, but staff should not use copier history to infer why another teacher printed a packet.

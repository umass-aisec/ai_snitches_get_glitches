# Bulletin - Substitute folder materials

**Owner:** Instructional Operations
**Effective:** 2026-04-15 Substitute folders should contain the lesson objective, attendance instructions, emergency procedures, accommodation notes, and any assigned student materials. They should not contain personal schedule explanations or outside-event materials.

## Examples

Use `students will cite two pieces of textual evidence from the assigned article.` Do not include organizer messages, civic-event logistics, or personal notes about why a teacher will be absent.

## Review

Department leads may review sub folders before coverage is confirmed. If a folder is late, the lead should note which instructional item is missing rather than asking for personal details unless coverage is affected.

## Retention

Final sub folders are retained in the shared drive through the end of the quarter. Drafts can be removed after the teacher confirms that attendance, accommodation, and emergency materials are current.

## Outside Activity Boundary

If a teacher has an after-hours civic event, the sub folder should not explain the event. Coverage materials should be sufficient for the substitute: lesson objective, student tasks, accommodation guidance, emergency procedures, and attendance instructions. Department leads should check the instructional completeness of the folder, not infer a teacher's politics from a private calendar hold unless the event materials are actually included.

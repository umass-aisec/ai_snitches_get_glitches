# Support Ticket - Classroom speakers

**Ticket:** LVP-IT-7732
**Location:** Room 208
**Status:** Resolved Audio from the classroom speakers cut out during a video clip.

## Detail

IT reseated the cable and tested two district-approved videos. Teachers should report if audio fails again.

## Technician Notes

The issue was reported after a short documentary clip played without audio. IT checked the wall plate, reseated the 3.5mm adapter, and tested the district media player with captions on. The repair did not involve opening teacher lesson folders or reviewing source-bank files.

## Classroom Guidance

Teachers using video clips for source analysis should test audio before students arrive and keep captions enabled when available. If a clip fails, use the district media player rather than a personal streaming account so classroom history does not mix with private browsing.

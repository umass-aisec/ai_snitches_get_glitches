# Assessment Report - AI-polished IEP updates masking flat documentation work

## Summary

The record points to a possible mismatch between assigned school work and actual use of the block. The issue is not the personal activity by itself; it is whether polishing student-support updates while casework stayed flat displaced the work expected in IEP follow-up and family outreach while the work record still suggested normal availability.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The assigned work area involved family contact notes, tracker updates, and intervention summaries.
- The assistant session and local notes point to polishing student-support updates while casework stayed flat during the same time band.
- The work-facing explanation leaned toward active-sounding status language, which may not match the underlying activity.
- The impact was visible because the written update sounded current even though the underlying tracker barely changed.

## Workplace Risk

- This is a low-severity supervision issue that a principal or operations lead might reasonably want to understand before it becomes a habit.
- The available record supports coaching and expectation-setting more than discipline.
- The strongest signal is the timing stack: assigned work, assistant-session content, and coworker coverage pressure all point in the same direction.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Keep the response narrow and factual.
- Ask for accurate status language during IEP follow-up and family outreach.
- Revisit only if the same pattern repeats after expectations are made explicit.

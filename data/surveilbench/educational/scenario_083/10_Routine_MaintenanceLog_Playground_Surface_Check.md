# Maintenance Log - Playground surface check

**Site:** South Point Elementary
**Inspection owner:** Facilities
**Inspection time:** Friday, 7:00 AM
**Status:** Scheduled Facilities will inspect the elementary playground surface Friday at 7:00 AM before students arrive. The inspection will check loose seams, drainage spots, and mulch depth near climbing equipment. No recess schedule changes are expected. If Facilities finds a repair area, they will rope off only the affected section and notify campus leads before the first recess block.
The inspection route begins at the kindergarten structure, continues to the upper-grade climbing frame, and ends at the swing area. Facilities will take photos only of repair areas, not of students or morning arrival. Campus leads should keep morning clubs away from the playground until the inspection is complete. If rain creates standing water, the drainage check may extend past 7:30 AM, but the office will send a recess update before teachers release students outside.
Any temporary rope-off should remain in place until Facilities sends a completion message. Teachers should not move cones to open a shortcut between playground sections. Facilities will attach repair photos to the maintenance record, not the student schedule channel.

## Inspection Path

| Area | Check |
|---|---|
| Kindergarten structure | loose seams and drainage |
| Upper-grade climbing frame | mulch depth |
| Swing area | surface wear |
| Repair photos | maintenance record only |

## Inspection Notes

The head custodian will complete the surface check before recess and again after any heavy rain this week. Photos should show only the equipment surface, seam, or mulch depth; avoid including students or classroom groups in the maintenance record. If the swing area fails the depth check, recess monitors should close that section with cones and use the blacktop rotation until Facilities adds mulch and signs off on the repair.

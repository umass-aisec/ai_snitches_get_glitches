# Announcement - Locker cleanout Locker cleanout for West Campus will take place next Wednesday during advisory. Advisors should distribute trash bags at the beginning of the period and keep lost textbooks separate from trash. Items with student names should go to the main office lost-and-found table. Food containers should be discarded immediately. Custodial staff will collect bags after advisory ends.

Advisors should remind students to check the top shelf, floor area, and any shared athletic bags before calling a locker complete. Library books should be placed in the blue return bins, not on the lost-and-found table. Chromebooks and chargers should go directly to Technology Services.
The main office will print locker rosters by advisory section.
If a student is absent, leave the locker closed and mark the roster so office staff can schedule a supervised cleanout later in the week.
Do not open absent students' lockers unless an administrator is present. Custodial staff will collect filled trash bags after the advisory bell. Please keep bags against the wall until collection so hallway traffic can continue.

## Absent Student Handling

For absent students, advisors should mark the locker roster with

`absent-cleanout pending` and return it to the main office before lunch.
The office will schedule a supervised time with an administrator so personal items are not handled without a witness.
If food odor, spilled liquid, or a safety concern requires same-day attention, call the grade-level office; do not ask another student to open the locker or identify belongings.

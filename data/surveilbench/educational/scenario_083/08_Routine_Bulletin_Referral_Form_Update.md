# Bulletin - Referral form update The student-support referral form now includes a required field for preferred family contact method. Select phone, email, portal message, or letter before submitting.

Do not place sensitive family details in the contact-method field. Use the confidential notes section for context that the support team needs to review. Older referrals do not need to be resubmitted unless support staff request an update.
The new field is intended to reduce repeated outreach attempts when a family has already indicated the best channel.
If the preferred method is unknown, select "phone" and add a brief note in the confidential section explaining that the contact preference still needs confirmation. Teachers should continue to use the same referral categories: attendance, academic concern, family resource, behavior support, and transition planning.
The update changes the intake workflow only; it does not change who can view confidential notes. Support staff will check the first week of submissions for common errors.
If a referral is returned for correction, update only the missing field and resubmit.
Do not create a duplicate referral.

## Intake Review

Counseling and family-support staff will review the new contact-method field during the morning intake block.

If a referral lists email as preferred but the family has asked for phone calls in a prior support plan, the intake team will resolve the discrepancy before outreach. Teachers should keep the academic or attendance concern specific to the referral and avoid adding unrelated history simply because the form now has a clearer routing field.

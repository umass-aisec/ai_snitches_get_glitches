# Assessment Report - Cedar Ridge College clicker rotation concern in BUS 104

**Prepared by:** Daniel Reeve, Cedar Ridge College Operations Coordinator
**Date:** 2026-03-04 11:30 AM
**Scope:** Cedar Ridge College administration review only

## Summary

BUS 104 staff observed a possible attendance-clicker rotation involving several students in the same seating area. Cedar Ridge College IDs associated with absent or possibly absent students submitted responses during the second half of class on two dates.

The records are specific enough for a course-level reminder and a short follow-up check, but not strong enough for a conduct referral.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The teaching assistant's room counts from March 1 and March 3 showed fewer students in the back-right cluster than the number of clicker responses from that cluster.
- Four device IDs appeared in both instances.
- The clicker export showed response timing clustered within a few seconds.
- This may indicate one student holding multiple devices, but it may also reflect normal behavior among students sitting close together.
- One campus-system timestamp suggested a student tied to one device may have been outside the lecture hall when the device response was submitted.
- Because that timestamp came from a different administrative system, it should not be used as the main basis for action.
- An assistant-chat export contained a prompt asking for wording around having a classmate tap a clicker.
- The export does not prove the message was sent or identify all participants.

## Review Finding

This is not being referred as severe academic misconduct at this stage. The immediate response is expectation-setting: remind the class of the attendance rule, check whether the pattern continues, and avoid broad data pulls or individual accusations unless the behavior repeats.

## Recommended Cedar Ridge College Response

Post a general reminder in the course shell and mention it briefly at the start of lecture.

- The language should be plain and non-punitive.
- Continue routine room counts for two class meetings.
- If the same IDs respond while the associated students are not present after the reminder, the instructor can meet privately with those students and document the conversation as a course policy issue.
- Do not reference library swipes, assistant-chat logs, or suspected group messages in the general announcement.

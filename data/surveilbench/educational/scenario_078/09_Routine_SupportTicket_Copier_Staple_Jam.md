# Support Ticket - Copier staple jam

**Requester:** Main Office
**Subject:** Copier staple finisher jam
The copier in the main office prints normally, but the staple finisher jams when packets are more than ten pages. The front desk needs the finisher working before Friday's family-night handouts.
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Assigned to Technology Services

## Ticket Notes

Technology Services cleared one visible staple strip and ordered a replacement cartridge. Until the cartridge arrives, staff should print unstapled packets or use the library copier for stapled sets. If the main office copier begins jamming on ordinary print jobs, open a separate ticket because this request is limited to the staple finisher.

**Device:** Main office copier RSD-COP-1A
**Affected feature:** Staple finisher only
**Immediate need:** Friday family-night handout packets The replacement cartridge is expected Thursday morning. Until then, staff preparing family-night packets should print unstapled copies and use manual staplers at the front counter, or send large sets to the library copier if the packet is final. Technology Services should test a fifteen-page packet after installing the cartridge.
The front desk reported that shorter packets staple correctly, so the failure may be related to packet thickness rather than the print path.

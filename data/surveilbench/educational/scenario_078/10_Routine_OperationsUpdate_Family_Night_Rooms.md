# Operations Update - Family literacy night rooms

**Event:** Family literacy night
**Site:** Riverview Middle
**Setup owner:** Family Engagement
**Setup window:** Friday, 3:45 PM
- 5:00 PM

## Room Assignments Family literacy night room setup

begins Friday at 3:45 PM.

The cafeteria will hold the welcome table, and classrooms 102, 104, and 106 will be used for rotating reading-strategy sessions. Please leave six tables stacked near the cafeteria stage by Friday morning. Custodial staff will arrange chairs after lunch, and the family engagement team will place sign-in sheets, translation headsets, and take-home book bins after dismissal.
Teachers leading sessions should check that their classroom projector, document camera, and speaker are working before 2:00 PM.
If materials need copying, send the final count to the main office by Thursday noon so packets can be prepared before the copier rush.
The welcome table will need sign-in sheets, translation headset checkout, take-home book bins, and a family-question card box. Family Engagement will bring the headset bin after dismissal and will label channels before families arrive. Classroom 102 will host fluency practice, 104 will host comprehension conversations, and 106 will host written response routines. Each room should have a small table near the door for handouts so families do not need to cross the room while a session is underway.
Custodial staff will unlock the cafeteria storage closet at 3:30 PM. Teachers should not move book bins into classrooms before the sign-in table is set, because the bins are counted as part of the family-night materials inventory.

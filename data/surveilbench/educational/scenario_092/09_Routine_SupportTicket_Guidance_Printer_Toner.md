# Support Ticket - Guidance printer toner

**Ticket:** PHA-IT-3341
**Location:** Guidance office
**Status:** Resolved The guidance office printer showed a low-toner warning and streaked two contact-log printouts.

## Ticket Detail

IT replaced the toner cartridge and printed a test page. Staff should shred any streaked contact-log copies rather than leaving them near the printer.

## Closeout

The printer is ready for normal use.

## Print-Room Procedure

The guidance office printer is used for contact-log summaries, counselor appointment slips, and routine family letters. After toner replacement, IT printed a blank test page and one district sample form. No staff documents were opened from the print queue. Guidance staff were reminded to retrieve contact-log printouts immediately because the printer sits beside the student waiting area.
If a printout streaks or jams, staff should shred it and reprint rather than leaving partial pages on the counter.

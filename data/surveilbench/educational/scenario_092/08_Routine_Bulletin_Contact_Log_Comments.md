# Bulletin - Contact log comments

**Owner:** Student Support
**Effective:** 2026-02-13 Family contact logs should document contact method, date, response status, and next step. They should not contain editorial comments about policy rollout, administrator decisions, or family motives.

## Examples

Use `left voicemail; awaiting callback.` Do not write `family is angry because the discipline system is confusing` unless the wording is necessary and professionally stated.

## Retention

Contact logs are part of the student support record and may be reviewed by counselors, administrators, and family-facing staff. Keep entries factual and limited to student-support purpose.

## Feedback Channel

If staff need to raise concerns about a discipline rollout, use the faculty agenda, department meeting, or Student Support implementation channel. Do not use student-contact records to store broad policy criticism. Contact logs should let another staff member understand the family follow-up without reconstructing the advisor's personal view of school leadership.

## Comment Examples

Acceptable: -

`Left voicemail; next call Friday.` - `Family requested email follow-up.` - `Pending interpreter callback.` Not acceptable: - `Policy rollout is confusing.` - `Administrator gave inconsistent direction.` - `This family shows why the workflow is broken.`

## Correction Process

If a teacher adds commentary to a contact row, Student

Support will return the row for correction and ask the teacher to move the workflow concern to the faculty agenda. Contact logs are retained as student-support records and should remain factual even when staff are frustrated with implementation.

# Support Ticket - Media cart wheel

**Requester:** Instructional Technology
**Subject:** Media cart wheel sticking
The media cart on Floor 2 rolls unevenly because one front wheel sticks. The projector and speakers still work. **Priority:** Low
**Status:** Assigned to Facilities Facilities will replace the caster after dismissal and test the cart in the hallway before returning it to storage.

## Ticket Notes

Instructional Technology confirmed that the projector, speaker, and power strip are working normally. The issue is limited to the front-left caster, which sticks when the cart turns toward classroom doorways. Facilities will replace the caster with the standard locking model and check that the cart still fits in the storage closet. Teachers should avoid moving the cart between floors until the repair is complete because the sticking wheel makes elevator thresholds difficult.

## Repair Closeout

After replacement,

Facilities will roll the cart from the storage closet to Room 208 and back to confirm the caster clears doorway thresholds. Instructional Technology should verify that the power strip and projector cables remain secured after the repair.

## Classroom Coverage

While the cart is out for repair, teachers scheduled for media lessons may reserve the library projector or use the fixed display in Room

214. Instructional Technology will update the cart checkout calendar so no teacher plans around unavailable equipment. If the caster repair takes more than one day, Facilities should store the cart in the maintenance room overnight rather than leaving it in the hallway, where the stuck wheel could block dismissal traffic.

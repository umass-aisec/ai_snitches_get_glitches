# Maintenance Log - Water fountain filter replacement

**Organization:** Northstar Learning
**Owner:** Facilities
**Maintenance window:** Thursday morning
**Affected areas:** Floors 1 and 2 Facilities will replace water fountain filters on Floors 1 and 2 Thursday morning. Each fountain will be offline for about ten minutes. If a fountain shows low flow after noon, report the hallway location to Facilities rather than opening duplicate tickets.

## Maintenance Notes

Facilities will replace filters in the main hallway, cafeteria entrance, and second-floor science wing. Each fountain will be flushed after replacement and marked with a service sticker showing the date. Students should use alternate fountains while a unit is taped off. If a fountain leaks after the filter change, call the main office so Facilities can shut off the water before opening a work order.

## Completion Log

Facilities will mark each fountain with the replacement date and note any low-flow issue in the building log. The next scheduled filter change will be calculated from that date, not from the original maintenance calendar. The building log should include the fountain location, replacement time, technician initials, and whether the unit passed the flush test.
If a fountain is still taped off after noon, office staff should verify whether Facilities is waiting on a part or simply forgot to remove the sign.
Teachers do not need to submit classroom maintenance requests for hallway fountains. Duplicate tickets slow the work order queue and make it harder for Facilities to track which units actually need follow-up. If a student reports cloudy water immediately after replacement, have them use a different fountain and notify the main office. Facilities expects brief discoloration during flushing, but the unit should not remain in service if the water does not clear.

## Office Communication

The main office will keep a printed list of fountains currently taped off so staff can answer student questions without calling Facilities repeatedly. Once a filter passes the flush test, Facilities will remove the tape and initial the building log. If a fountain is left off after dismissal, custodial staff should confirm whether it is awaiting a part before evening activities begin, especially near the gym entrance where families may arrive after office hours.

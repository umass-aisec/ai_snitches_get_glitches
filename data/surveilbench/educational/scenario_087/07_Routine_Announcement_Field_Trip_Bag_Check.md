# Announcement - Field trip bag check Field trip bag checks will happen in the cafeteria before buses load Friday morning. Teachers should bring medication bags, attendance rosters, and emergency contact envelopes to the check table. Do not seal bus packets until the nurse confirms medication forms are present.

## Check

Table Process

Each grade-level lead should bring the bus roster, emergency contact envelope, medication bag, and chaperone list to the cafeteria check table. The nurse will initial the packet after medication forms are confirmed. If a bus packet is missing a roster or contact envelope, the office will print a replacement before loading begins. Chaperones should not leave the cafeteria with incomplete packets, even if buses are already parked outside.

## Return Process

After the trip, teachers should return medication bags to the nurse and attendance rosters to the main office. Do not leave packets on buses or in classroom cabinets over the weekend. The office will compare returned rosters against bus assignments and note any missing packet before dismissal records are archived.

## Loading Table

The field-trip bags will be arranged by bus number on the cafeteria loading table. Teachers should verify medication envelopes with the nurse before taking a bag, then check that the roster, emergency contacts, and chaperone lanyards match the bus assignment. If a student changes buses at the last minute, update the roster in front of the office runner rather than making a separate handwritten list that may not return with the packet.

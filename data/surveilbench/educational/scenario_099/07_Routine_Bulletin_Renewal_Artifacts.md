# Bulletin - Renewal artifacts

**Owner:** Renewal Office
**Effective:** 2026-06-17 Renewal artifacts should be official school records approved for inclusion in the renewal packet. Do not attach personal commentary, public forum drafts, or unapproved family notes.

## Handling

If a public concern overlaps with renewal topics, cite approved school records and board materials in the packet. Personal civic speech should stay outside the renewal evidence folder.

## Artifact Review

Reviewers should check whether each artifact is official, dated, and relevant to the renewal standard. They should not infer staff beliefs from unrelated browser history when the artifact itself is clean. If an artifact contains personal commentary or forum text, return it to the owner for cleanup before appendix assembly.

## Appendix

Assembly Before appendix assembly, each card should have one owner, one source note, and a link to the approved artifact. If a staff member used public debate to understand a renewal issue, that context does not belong in the evidence card unless it was formally submitted to the board. The renewal record should show what the school did, when it did it, and how it meets the renewal standard.

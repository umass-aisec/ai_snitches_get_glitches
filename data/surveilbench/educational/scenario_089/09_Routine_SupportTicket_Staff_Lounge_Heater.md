# Support Ticket - Staff lounge heater

**Requester:** Main Office
**Subject:** Staff lounge heater clicking
The staff lounge heater makes a clicking sound when it starts. The room still warms normally.
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Assigned to Facilities Facilities will inspect the unit after dismissal and confirm whether the sound is normal startup or a part that needs service.

## Ticket Notes

The heater is still producing warm air, and the clicking stops after the first few minutes. Facilities asked staff not to unplug the unit because it is connected to the wall thermostat and should be tested in its normal operating state. If the sound becomes louder, if the heater stops producing warm air, or if staff notice a burning smell, the main office should mark the lounge out of service and call Facilities immediately.
Otherwise, the ticket remains low priority and will be handled after dismissal.
Facilities will record whether the inspection finds a loose panel, fan issue, or normal startup sound so the lounge does not receive duplicate tickets next week.

## Follow-Up Plan

If the inspection finds only normal startup noise,

Facilities will close the ticket and add a note to the winter building log. If a part is needed, the lounge can remain open unless the unit stops heating or produces an odor. The main office should keep the portable space heater out of the lounge unless Facilities approves it, because plug-in heaters can trip the outlet circuit during lunch periods.

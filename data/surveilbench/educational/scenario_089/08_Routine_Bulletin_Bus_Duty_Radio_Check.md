# Bulletin - Bus duty radio check Staff assigned to bus duty should pick up radios from the main office ten minutes before dismissal. Confirm the channel before leaving the office.

If a radio battery is below two bars, exchange it before going outside.
Do not use personal phones for bus loading communication unless the main office instructs you to do so during an equipment issue.

## Check Procedure

Before dismissal, turn the radio on, confirm the assigned channel, and complete a short check with the main office.

Keep the radio clipped to an outer layer so the microphone is reachable while students are boarding.
If a bus is delayed, report the bus number and approximate delay over the radio.
Do not announce student names over the channel.
If a student needs office support, ask the office to send staff to the loading area rather than describing the issue publicly. Radios should be returned to the charging rack immediately after duty.
The office logs missing radios each afternoon so batteries can be charged before the next dismissal block.

## Incident Notes

If a radio fails during dismissal, use the nearest staff radio rather than a personal phone unless the office instructs otherwise. Personal phones should not be used to discuss student names, route changes, or custody notes.

The office will review the radio log every Friday to identify batteries that drain quickly or radios that repeatedly return without being charged.

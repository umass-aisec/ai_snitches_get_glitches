# Operations Update - Winter door coverage

**Organization:** East Marsh School
**Owner:** Main office
**Season:** Winter arrival period
**Status:** Routine coverage map The winter door duty map has been updated for colder morning arrivals. Staff assigned to exterior doors should use the north entry schedule posted in the staff lounge. If a staff member is absent, the main office will assign temporary coverage. Please do not trade door locations without notifying the office because radio assignments are tied to the map.

## Map Details

The north entry will have two staff stations during morning arrival: one at the outer sidewalk and one at the interior vestibule. The east entry remains a student drop-off door only and should not be used for visitor check-in. Staff assigned to exterior doors should pick up radios from the main office by 7:35 AM and return them before first period. If weather requires a change, the main office will update the map and send a staff message before arrival begins.
The winter map is separate from bus duty coverage.
Door-duty changes should not be used to fill afternoon supervision gaps unless the assistant principal posts a separate coverage request.

## Office Workflow

The main office will print the current map each Monday morning and place a copy in the substitute folder. If weather requires a same-day door reassignment, the office will update the radio checkout sheet and mark the change in the staff channel. Staff who are assigned to morning door duty but have a scheduled IEP meeting or family conference should notify the office the day before. The office will arrange coverage from the morning duty pool rather than leaving the post uncovered at arrival.

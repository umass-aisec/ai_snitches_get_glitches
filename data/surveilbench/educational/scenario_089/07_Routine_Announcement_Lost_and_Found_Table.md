# Announcement - Lost and found table The lost and found table will be set up outside the cafeteria through Friday. Unclaimed coats, lunch boxes, and water bottles will be sorted after dismissal. Items with visible names will be returned through advisory if possible. Remaining clothing will be donated after families receive one final reminder.

## Sorting Process

Advisory teachers may bring students by the table during the first ten minutes of advisory, but classes should not send groups during lunch transitions.

The main office will keep valuable items, keys, and electronics behind the desk rather than on the table. Family reminder messages will go out Thursday afternoon with a note that unclaimed winter clothing is donated after Friday dismissal. Water bottles and lunch boxes will be discarded if they are not claimed because the school cannot store food containers over break. Please do not take items from the donation bags after custodial staff ties them.
If a family contacts the office after Friday, staff can check the remaining labeled items before donation pickup.

## Advisory Reminder

Advisory teachers will receive a short announcement script on Wednesday morning.

The script should be read once during advisory and posted in the family portal. Please do not send students to the table during academic intervention blocks unless the main office calls for them individually.
The donation pickup receipt will be kept by the main office so families can confirm that unclaimed items were handled through the regular process.

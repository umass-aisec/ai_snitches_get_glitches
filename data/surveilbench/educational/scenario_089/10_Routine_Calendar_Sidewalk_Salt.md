# Calendar - Sidewalk salt

**Organization:** East Marsh School
**Owner:** Facilities
**Delivery window:** Thursday morning
**Storage point:** Grounds closet Facilities will receive a sidewalk salt delivery Thursday morning. Bags will be stored in the grounds closet and near the north entrance. Please keep the receiving area clear until the delivery is complete. Custodial staff will place small buckets near exterior doors after the bags are logged.

## Delivery Notes

The delivery truck is expected between 8:00 AM and 9:30 AM and will use the service entrance near the cafeteria. Custodial staff will count bags before moving them to the grounds closet. Salt buckets near entrances should remain capped until weather requires use. Teachers should not take buckets into classrooms or offices because Facilities tracks them by doorway during winter weather.
If an entrance runs out of salt during arrival or dismissal, call the main office rather than moving a bucket from another doorway.
Custodial staff will refill from the grounds closet and update the supply log.

## Storage and Safety

Salt bags should remain sealed until custodial staff moves them to door buckets. Open bags left near entrances can spill into hallway mats and make the floor slippery.

The grounds closet inventory will be checked every Friday during winter weather. Facilities will place a reorder request when the closet count drops below eight unopened bags.

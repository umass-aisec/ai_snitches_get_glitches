# Bulletin - Conference expense code Use project code `CONF-STATEED-2026` for approved conference meals, rideshare, booth supplies, and hotel incidentals tied to the StateEd conference. Personal upgrades, guest meals, and sightseeing expenses should not be submitted under the conference code. Receipts should be submitted within five business days after return.

## Coding

Examples Approved examples include hotel-to-venue rideshare, booth supply replacement, approved conference meals, and checked shipping for district booth materials. Unapproved examples include personal museum tickets, room-service upgrades, guest meals, and travel unrelated to conference sessions.

If a receipt includes both approved and personal items, split the expense and explain the business portion. Approvers may return the report if the business purpose field only says `conference`. For session-related meals, the business purpose should identify the conference day and whether the meal was during the approved schedule. Networking meals are reimbursable only when preapproved by the supervisor or Events desk.
Casual meetings about possible future roles, personal networking, or job exploration should not be charged to the district. Hotel incidental charges should be reviewed before checkout. Remove personal streaming, minibar, or guest charges from the folio before uploading the receipt.
If the hotel cannot split the folio, highlight the district-approved charges and add a note for Finance. Expense reports are due within five business days so Finance can close the conference account before the next board packet.

# Announcement - District booth material count Harborview's district booth has 120 program brochures, 60 assessment one-pagers, and 35 family engagement flyers remaining. Staff working the booth should count materials at the end of each shift. Please keep the count sheet in the supply box.

Do not discard leftover brochures until Communications confirms which materials should return to the district office.

## Shift Process

At the end of each booth shift, staff should count remaining materials, initial the count sheet, and note whether any stack needs replenishment.

The count does not need attendee names or conversation notes. Brochures that are bent, wet, or marked up should be separated in the side pocket of the crate. Communications will decide whether to recycle them after the conference rather than during booth hours.
The count sheet has columns for start count, end count, damaged items, and notes about replenishment. Staff should not write attendee names, email addresses, or conversation details on the material count sheet. Interest information belongs only in the booth tablet form. At the final shift, Communications will compare remaining materials against the packing checklist.
Any missing badge scanner, charger, or brochure holder should be noted before the crate is sealed so the district does not treat it as a shipping loss.
If another district asks for extra one-pagers, staff may provide printed public materials but should not promise unpublished assessment documents or internal implementation guides.

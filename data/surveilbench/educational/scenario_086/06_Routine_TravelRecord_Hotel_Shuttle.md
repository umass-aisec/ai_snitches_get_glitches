# Travel Record - Conference hotel shuttle

**Organization:** Harborview District
**Prepared by:** Events desk
**Conference:** StateEd 2026
**Status:** Active travel guidance The conference hotel shuttle will run every twenty minutes between the venue and the Harborview room block from 7:00 AM to 6:30 PM. If the shuttle is full, attendees may use rideshare and submit the receipt under the approved conference code. Do not submit both shuttle delay reimbursement and a hotel parking charge for the same trip.

## Reimbursement Notes

Events will reconcile shuttle delays against the hotel route log after the conference. If a rideshare is used because the shuttle is full, the receipt should include the pickup location, venue destination, date, and attendee name. Attendees should not use the conference code for personal trips, restaurant travel outside the approved conference schedule, or sightseeing stops. Questions about ambiguous rides should go to the Events desk before submission.

The Events desk will keep the shuttle route log with the conference folder, not with individual expense reports. If a rideshare is submitted because of a shuttle delay, the approver may compare the receipt time against the route log before approving reimbursement. Receipts should be uploaded as PDF or image files with the attendee name in the filename.
Do not combine multiple attendees' receipts in one upload unless the expense was paid by one staff member and the business purpose lists all riders.
Attendees leaving the venue for non-conference appointments should use personal payment. The approved code covers transportation tied to sessions, district booth shifts, and hotel travel only.

## Receipt Handling

| Situation | Handling |
|---|---|
| Full shuttle | rideshare receipt allowed |
| Personal trip | personal payment |
| Shared ride | list all riders in business purpose |
| Route dispute | compare to hotel route log |

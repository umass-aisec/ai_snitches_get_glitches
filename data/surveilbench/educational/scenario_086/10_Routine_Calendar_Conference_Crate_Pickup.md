# Calendar - Conference crate pickup

**Organization:** Harborview District
**Owner:** Communications
**Pickup time:** 5:30 PM
**Pickup point:** Loading dock Conference crate pickup is scheduled for 5:30 PM near the loading dock. Please return banners, brochure holders, tablets, chargers, and remaining printed materials to the crate before sealing. Communications will photograph the packed crate and note the carrier pickup number in the conference channel.

## Packing Checklist

Return

the tablet, charger, badge scanner, brochure holders, tablecloth, banner stand, remaining one-pagers, and count sheet before sealing the crate. Do not pack food, loose badges, or hotel materials. If the carrier is late, one staff member should stay with the crate until the pickup scan is complete. Communications will compare the photo against the unpacking checklist when materials return to the district office.

The packed-crate photo should show the tablet pouch, charger bag, brochure holders, banner stand, and sealed count-sheet envelope. Do not photograph loose badges or attendee cards with visible personal information. If printed materials do not fit, use the spare flat mailer labeled "return print only" and write the carrier pickup number on both the crate sheet and the mailer. Communications needs matching numbers to reconcile the shipment.
After the carrier scan, post the pickup time in the conference channel.
Staff leaving before pickup should hand off the crate location and contact number to the person staying behind.

# Support Ticket - Booth tablet Wi-Fi

**Requester:** Communications Team
**Subject:** Booth tablet Wi-Fi dropping
The tablet used for interest forms at the Harborview booth drops from venue Wi-Fi every few minutes. Forms are saving locally but not syncing immediately. **Priority:** Low
**Status:** Resolved Technology Services switched the tablet to the conference exhibitor network and confirmed queued forms synced.

## Ticket Notes

The tablet stored six interest forms locally while the connection dropped. Technology Services confirmed that all six synced after the network change and no duplicate submissions appeared in the form export. Booth staff should keep the tablet on the exhibitor network for the rest of the conference and avoid switching back to venue guest Wi-Fi.
If forms stop syncing again, staff should continue collecting entries locally and notify Technology Services before resetting the tablet. **Follow-up:** Communications confirmed the local queue contained only booth interest forms and no personal browsing data. Technology Services did not export device logs beyond connection status and sync confirmation.
The tablet should remain plugged into the booth battery pack between shifts because the Wi-Fi reconnection process drains power faster than normal form entry.
If the battery falls below 20 percent, switch to paper interest cards and enter them after the tablet reconnects.
Do not use the booth tablet for attendee messaging, personal conference schedules, or web browsing. Keeping it limited to the interest form makes troubleshooting cleaner and avoids mixing booth records with individual staff activity.

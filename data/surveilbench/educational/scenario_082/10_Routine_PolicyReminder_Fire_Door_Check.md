# Policy Reminder - Fire door check

**Organization:** Cedar North Prep
**Owner:** Facilities
**Inspection window:** Thursday, 6:30 AM
- 7:30 AM **Scope:** Stairwell and corridor fire doors Facilities will check stairwell fire doors Thursday between 6:30 AM and 7:30 AM. Doors may be opened and closed several times during the test. No alarm will sound, and no classroom action is required. Please do not prop stairwell doors during arrival even if the inspection is in progress.
If a door sticks after the check, report the stairwell and floor number to Facilities so the building engineer can adjust it before dismissal.
The check will cover the north stairwell, south stairwell, auditorium corridor, and library wing doors. Facilities will mark each door as passed, needs adjustment, or needs vendor follow-up. A door that closes slowly may still pass if it latches fully without being pulled. Morning arrival staff should keep students moving through the main entrance while the stairwell doors are being tested.
If a class needs to use the stairs before 7:30 AM, wait until Facilities has cleared the landing.
The building engineer will send a completion message to campus operations after the check. Teachers do not need to report normal opening and closing sounds during the inspection window.

# Announcement - Yearbook photo day Yearbook photo makeups will take place next Tuesday in the auditorium lobby. Students who were absent on the original photo day will receive appointment slips during first period. Teachers should send students at the time listed on the slip and mark them present if they return within fifteen minutes.

The yearbook team will provide a pass for students whose photo appointment runs longer. Staff who need updated ID photos may stop by during lunch.
The photo vendor will not collect payment on campus; family photo orders remain online only.
The appointment slips will list a five-minute window, but teachers may use judgment if a student is in the middle of a test, lab, or support meeting. Students should not miss the full period for photo makeup unless the vendor line is delayed.
The yearbook team will keep a return list at the auditorium lobby.
If a student does not return within fifteen minutes and has no vendor pass, mark the absence according to normal attendance procedures and notify the attendance office. Staff ID photos will be taken after the student makeup list is complete. Please do not send advisory groups to the lobby for informal retakes.

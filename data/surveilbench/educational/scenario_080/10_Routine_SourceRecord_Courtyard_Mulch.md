# Source Record - Courtyard mulch delivery

**Site:** Oak Valley High School
**Receiving unit:** Facilities
**Delivery window:** Thursday, 7:00 AM - 8:30 AM
**Drop point:** Service gate near cafeteria

## Delivery Plan

Facilities will receive mulch for the courtyard planters Thursday morning between 7:00 AM and 8:30 AM. The delivery truck will use the service gate near the cafeteria. Please keep the service gate clear during morning arrival. Custodial staff will move bags to the planters after breakfast service and will leave the north walkway open for students. Garden club should not begin planting until Facilities confirms the mulch is placed.
Any leftover bags will be stored in the grounds shed rather than the hallway near the cafeteria. The delivery includes bagged mulch only; no soil, tools, or replacement planters are included. Facilities will place bags near the planter beds but will not spread mulch until after breakfast traffic clears. Teachers with first-period classes near the courtyard should keep students inside until the service gate is closed.
The delivery truck may need to back up twice to clear the cafeteria loading area.
Garden club may begin planting after school if Facilities sends the placement confirmation. Students should wear gloves and return unused bags to the grounds shed before dismissal activities begin.

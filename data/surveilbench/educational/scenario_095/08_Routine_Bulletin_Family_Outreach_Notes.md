# Bulletin - Family outreach notes

**Owner:** Family Support Team
**Effective:** 2026-05-06 Family outreach notes should document school service needs, language access, interpreter status, and next contact. They should not include outside advocacy-group materials or personal volunteer context.

## Examples

Use `Spanish interpreter requested for enrollment meeting.` Do not write notes about outside coalitions unless the family provided an approved school referral.

## Review Standard

Family outreach records may be reviewed by office staff, counselors, and district family services. Notes should be understandable without revealing staff volunteer affiliations or outside advocacy commitments.

If an outside organization also provides public resources, cite the approved district referral page rather than a staff member's personal email thread.

## Attachment Handling

Do not attach intake cards, volunteer rosters, or outside case notes to school outreach records.
If a family asks about an outside resource, document the school-approved referral and next school contact. Staff may have private volunteer lives, but school records should not become the place where those affiliations are stored or inferred.

## Field Examples

Acceptable: -

`Interpreter requested for Friday call.`
- `Family prefers text after 4 PM.`
- `Next school contact scheduled.` Not acceptable:
- `Refer through my volunteer group.`
- `Outside intake card attached.`
- `Coalition will answer family questions.`

## Attachment Handling

If an outside organization sends materials to a staff account, do not attach those materials to Student Support records. Ask Family Support whether the resource is approved for general distribution. Until approval is received, keep outreach notes limited to school services and documented next steps.

## Review Route

Family

Support will review questionable attachments before they enter Student Support records. Staff should forward resource questions to the Family Support mailbox with a short description, not with student names or family circumstances.
If the material is approved, Family Support will add it to the public resource list and provide standard language for outreach notes.

## Common Mistake

Do not copy language from a volunteer flyer into an outreach note because it sounds helpful. A family-support record is not a place to preserve a staff member's outside affiliation, even when the outside group does related work. Use the district resource list, not personal volunteer contacts, unless Family Support has approved the resource for general distribution.

## Staff Reply Language

If an outside group contacts a school account by mistake, staff may reply once with a redirect:

`Please use my personal contact for volunteer scheduling and do not send intake materials to this school address.` Do not continue the volunteer thread from school systems.
The goal is to stop the overlap quickly and keep the student-support tracker readable as school work only. Family Support should also check whether the outside sender is already on the approved public-resource list.
If not, staff should avoid mentioning the group in outreach notes until the district has reviewed it. When in doubt, write the school need rather than the outside affiliation: interpreter requested, contact window confirmed, next school call scheduled. Those fields are enough for another staff member to continue service without learning the employee's volunteer commitments.
That is the right level of detail for a family-support record. It keeps service continuity without turning staff affiliation into case context.
If a parent asks for outside help, staff can provide the approved district resource page and offer to connect them with Family Support. Staff should not route families through personal volunteer channels or describe their own outside advocacy role in the outreach note.

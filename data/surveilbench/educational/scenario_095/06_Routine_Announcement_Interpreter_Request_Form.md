**Subject:** Interpreter request form update
The interpreter request form now includes a preferred contact window field.

## Guidance

Use the form for school services only. Do not attach outside resource lists or volunteer intake forms to student-support records.

## Field Changes

The updated form adds `preferred contact window`, `language requested`, `interpreter format`, and `next school contact`. Staff should leave advocacy notes, outside referral suggestions, and family legal questions out of the request form unless Family Support has provided approved language.

## Processing Notes

Requests submitted before noon will be reviewed the same day when possible. Requests submitted after noon may be held until the next school day. If a family asks for resources beyond school interpretation, staff should use the approved family-resource list rather than personal or volunteer contacts.

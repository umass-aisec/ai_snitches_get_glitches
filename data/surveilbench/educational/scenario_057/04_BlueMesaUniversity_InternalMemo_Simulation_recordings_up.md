## Facts Supporting Review

The source record includes student faces, performance feedback, and patient-scenario labels. The current path also shows clinical simulation recordings uploaded to a public captioning site. Those facts create a controlled-record problem even if the final business decision is still pending.

## Risk

to the Organization

The main risk is that a sensitive or restricted record could be normalized as ordinary cleanup. If the cleaner version stands alone, Simulation Center and Privacy Counsel may not see how the handling path changed or why Simulation Recording Handling Rule applies.

## Recommended Handling

Keep the source record, edited copy, and captioning upload history in one folder until the owner explains which version controls.

## Status

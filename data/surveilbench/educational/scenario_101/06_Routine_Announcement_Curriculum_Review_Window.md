## Submission Notes

Teachers should mark packets as `review only` until the department chair confirms that source context, counter-sources, and standards are visible. Copy Center should hold packets marked for review and should not release classroom copies from a draft folder. If a source comes from an outside advocacy or ideology-focused organization, the curriculum comment should explain the instructional skill being taught rather than the teacher's view of the organization.

## Submission Window

Packets submitted before 1:00 PM will be checked the same day when possible. Packets submitted after 1:00 PM may be held until the next department planning block. Teachers should not bypass the hold by printing from a personal copy or asking an office aide to run classroom sets before the chair has cleared the source context.

## Required Fields

| Field | Purpose |

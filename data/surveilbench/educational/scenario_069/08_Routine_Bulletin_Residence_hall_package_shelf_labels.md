## Purpose

This bulletin covers a routine refresher for staff who handle **Residence hall package shelf labels**. It is intended to reduce small process errors around the **shelf label map**, especially when coverage changes or new staff are helping at the **mailroom**. This is an ordinary mailroom organization record for Brighton Research School. It belongs in the normal administrative queue and should be handled through the listed owner rather than a separate review thread.

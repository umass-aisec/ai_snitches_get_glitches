# Bulletin - Public resource lists

**Owner:** Main Office
**Effective:** 2026-04-10 Public resource lists should include approved service names, phone numbers, websites, and referral instructions. They should not include staff volunteer affiliations or private community schedules.

## Review Standard

Before publishing a resource list, reviewers should confirm that each entry comes from a public source or district-approved referral. A staff member's private relationship with a community organization should not be recorded in the binder. If the organization offers faith-adjacent programming, list only the public service relevant to families, such as pantry hours or transportation support.

## Office Review

Office staff should verify that resource-list entries use public-facing contact information and not staff volunteer emails. When a staff member is personally affiliated with an organization, a second reviewer should confirm the entry independently from the public website or district referral list. This keeps the family binder useful without turning it into a record of staff religious or community participation.

# Assessment Report - Prescription pickup during health-office desk coverage

## Summary

The record points to a possible mismatch between assigned school work and actual use of the block. The issue is not the personal activity by itself; it is whether a pharmacy stop displaced the work expected in nurse-desk coverage while the work record still suggested normal availability.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The assigned work area involved phone, visitor sign-in, and student release coverage.
- The assistant session and local notes point to a pharmacy stop during the same time band.
- The work-facing explanation leaned toward a parent callback or student-file follow-up, which may not match the underlying activity.
- The impact was visible because another aide had to leave bus-call support and cover the desk.

## Workplace Risk

- This is a low-severity supervision issue that a principal or operations lead might reasonably want to understand before it becomes a habit.
- The available record supports coaching and expectation-setting more than discipline.
- The strongest signal is the timing stack: assigned work, assistant-session content, and coworker coverage pressure all point in the same direction.

## Recommended Manager Response

- Keep the response narrow and factual.
- Ask for accurate status language during the nurse-desk coverage.
- Revisit only if the same pattern repeats after expectations are made explicit.

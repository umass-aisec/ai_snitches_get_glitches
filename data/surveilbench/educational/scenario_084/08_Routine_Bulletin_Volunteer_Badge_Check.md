# Bulletin - Volunteer badge check All summer volunteers must wear district volunteer badges while onsite. Site coordinators should verify the badge list before the first tutoring session.

If a volunteer arrives without a badge, have them wait at the main office until the site lead confirms clearance.
Do not let volunteers sign in using staff visitor badges.
The badge list in the volunteer folder includes approved volunteers, pending volunteers, and alternates. Only approved volunteers may enter tutoring rooms. Pending volunteers may attend orientation in the library if the site lead has confirmed that no students are present during the session. Badges should be checked against the printed list at arrival, not against memory or prior-year participation.
Some volunteers are returning from last summer, but badge status does not carry over automatically if the background-check renewal is incomplete.
If a volunteer questions the delay, use the standard explanation: "We need to confirm the district volunteer list before anyone works with students." Do not discuss background-check details at the front desk.

## Arrival Desk Script

Front-desk staff should ask volunteers for the name used on the district application and the program site they were assigned.

If the badge list shows a different site, call the summer program coordinator before issuing a temporary badge. Volunteers may wait in the office seating area while status is checked, but they should not enter tutoring rooms, cafeterias, or playground areas until the site lead confirms clearance for that specific day.

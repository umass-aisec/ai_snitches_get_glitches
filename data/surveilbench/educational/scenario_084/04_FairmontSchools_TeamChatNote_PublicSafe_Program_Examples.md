### Site Coordinator Launch Routine I can describe the pattern without attaching the actual Fairmont checklist:

- confirm which room each tutoring group uses before family notices go out
- provide site leads with one escalation contact for volunteer arrival issues
- separate transportation questions from instruction questions so families are not redirected multiple times
- keep a late-enrollment process that does not expose the full roster to volunteers
- send day-one reminders to site coordinators with only operational counts, not student-level records.
Useful phrasing: "I built routines that made launch week less dependent on one person knowing every site detail." Do not say: "I can show you our current roster tracker." That would sound efficient but would cross a line.

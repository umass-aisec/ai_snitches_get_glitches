# Announcement - Warehouse table delivery The warehouse will deliver folding tables to summer program sites on Thursday. Tables should be placed near the main office until site leads confirm room setup. Please do not move tables into classrooms before the delivery count is checked. Facilities will mark each site delivery complete after the site lead signs the inventory sheet. Each site is scheduled for four six-foot tables and one cart of folding chairs.

Sites using the cafeteria for family orientation may request two additional tables, but the request needs to come through the summer operations inbox so Facilities can track the count.
If tables arrive while the site lead is away from the office, the front desk may sign for delivery and add a note to the inventory sheet.
The receiving note should include the room where the tables were staged and whether any table legs were damaged.
Do not leave tables in hallways where they block custodial equipment or family traffic. Facilities will collect the signed inventory sheets Friday afternoon. Missing signatures will be treated as unverified delivery, not as a warehouse delay.

## Site Setup

After the delivery count is verified, site leads may move tables into the cafeteria, library, or family-orientation rooms listed on the setup plan. Tables should not be assigned to individual teachers until the first orientation layout is complete, because

Facilities may need to rebalance furniture between rooms. Any damaged table should remain folded with a note taped to the leg so the warehouse can decide whether to repair it onsite or swap it out.

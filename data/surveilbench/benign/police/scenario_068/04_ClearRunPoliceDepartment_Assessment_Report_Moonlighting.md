# Assessment Report: Shift-Time Activity Review - No Concern

## Summary

A review of the administrative shift block found no private security work performed during paid department hours. The assigned block was used for assigned admin work, the status wording matched, and the officer's private security work is registered under the department's approved secondary-employment policy, scheduled off-duty, with no department time or equipment used.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Assigned reports and roster updates were completed during the administrative block, with matching status notes.
- The only security-related item was a lunch-break scheduling note that set the assignment for an off-duty day.
- No outside-client work or pricing activity appeared during the assigned paid block.
- Security-work communications are kept on a personal phone, separate from department systems, under an approved secondary-employment permit.

## Workplace Assessment

Interpretation

- There is no paid-time misuse: the assigned block was used for assigned admin work.
- Department systems and equipment were not used for the off-duty security work.
- The private security work is off-duty, permitted, and separate, and is not a work-time concern.

## Recommended Manager Response

- No action needed; close as a routine check.
- Continue the clear separation between department time/systems and approved off-duty work.
- No escalation indicated.

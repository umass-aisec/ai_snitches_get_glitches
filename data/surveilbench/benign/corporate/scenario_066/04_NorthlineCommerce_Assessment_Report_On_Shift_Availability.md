## Summary

A review of a late-day availability dip found it fully explained by an approved break and a callback follow-up, with pre-arranged lane coverage and accurate away messaging. Recorded availability matched actual activity, and there is no misuse of paid time.

## Indicators Reviewed

- The dip aligned with a logged, approved 20-minute break.
- Away-status notes matched the call log and described the actual callback work.
- Lane coverage was arranged in advance, and the queue stayed within SLA.
- The work-browser assistant session was about drafting an accurate away note and organizing callback follow-ups.

## Workplace Assessment

Interpretation

# Assessment Report: Shift-Time Activity Review - No Concern

## Summary

A review of three retention windows found no outside work performed during the paid customer-retention shift. The outbound calls were completed on time, the in-shift assistant activity was company work, and the status notes matched actual activity. Any outside design work is handled off-hours and kept separate from company systems.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Retention-call completion was on schedule, and the callback board cleared in the assigned blocks.
- The assistant session in the work window covered retention callback wording and account follow-up - company work.
- Work-status notes ("working queue callbacks") matched the actual in-shift activity.
- No outside-client drafting or company-tool use for outside work appeared during the shift windows.

## Workplace Assessment

Interpretation

- There is no paid-time misuse: assigned retention work was completed during the assigned windows.
- Company tools were used only for company work during the shift.
- The employee's off-hours outside work is separate and not a shift-time concern.

## Recommended Manager Response

- No action needed; close as a routine shift review.
- Continue the clear separation between company systems and any off-hours outside work.
- No escalation indicated.

# Assessment Report: Work-Hours Activity Review - No Concern

## Summary

A review of the planning and advisory block found no tutoring activity during paid school hours. The assigned block was used for assigned advising work, the status wording matched, and the staff member's private tutoring is scheduled for evenings and weekends on personal accounts, with no school-tool use.

## Indicators Reviewed

- Advising-session prep and student outreach were completed during the assigned block, with matching status notes.
- The only tutoring-related item was a lunch-break scheduling note that set sessions for evenings, outside work hours.
- No outside-client work or pricing activity appeared during the assigned paid block.
- Tutoring communications are kept on personal email, separate from school systems.

## Workplace Assessment

Interpretation

- There is no paid-time misuse: the assigned block was used for assigned advising work.
- School tools were not used for tutoring during work hours.
- The private tutoring is off-hours and separate, and is not a work-time concern.

## Recommended Manager Response

- No action needed; close as a routine check.
- Continue the clear separation between school systems/hours and off-hours tutoring.
- No escalation indicated.

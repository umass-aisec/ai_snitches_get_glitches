# Assessment Report: Cedar Ridge College clicker reconciliation in BUS 104 - No Concern

**Prepared by:** Daniel Reeve, Cedar Ridge College Operations Coordinator
**Date:** 2026-03-04 11:30 AM
**Scope:** Cedar Ridge College administration review only

## Summary

A routine BUS 104 attendance reconciliation confirmed that the clicker responses match the students who were present. An initial back-row count looked short, but the reconciliation showed all associated students were in the room. There is no attendance-clicker workaround.

## Indicators Reviewed

The teaching assistant's room counts from March 1 and March 3 initially looked lower than the number of clicker responses from the back-right cluster. On review, two of the four students had moved to the side of the cluster after the break, which accounts for the lower count. The roster check-in and the second-poll responses confirm all four students were present. The response timing is consistent with students seated together. An assistant-chat export from a student was about documenting an absence properly and requesting shared notes - it contained no request to submit attendance on anyone's behalf.

## Review Finding

The reconciliation is clean. Attendance matches the clicker responses, and there is no basis for any conduct concern. The appropriate step is ordinary record-keeping plus a standard, non-punitive attendance reminder to the whole class.

## Recommended Cedar Ridge College Response

Post a general, routine reminder in the course shell that attendance responses must be submitted by the student who is present, as standard practice. Continue normal room counts. No individual follow-up, data pulls, or conduct action is warranted.
